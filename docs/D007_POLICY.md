Accepted for implementation and independent verification on 2026-11-02 UTC. Actual admission remains gated on code verification and review of the exact event annotation. ## Decision, rationale and limits

Accept a narrow domestic common-stock integer forward-split profile. Model ordinary economic holdings as one package carrying the attached split interest before the first adjusted session, then transform that package once into new units. Preserve all due-bill and provider metadata. No separate split receivable, broker delivery ledger or settlement cash proration is added. Existing numerical engine behavior remains unchanged unless review discovers an arithmetic defect.

This follows five separately briefed advisers: `/root/d007_accounting` (GPT-6 Astra), `/root/d007_sources` and `/root/d007_integrity` (GPT-6.1 Sol), `/root/d007_operations` and `/root/d007_scope` (GPT-6 Luna). Their reports and actual completed Fable5.1/Opus5.5 terminal critiques are archived under `docs/reviews/D007-*`. These are distinct AI processes, not statistically independent evidence or five human endorsements.

The model deliberately uses economic cash and positions. It does not claim settled inventory, broker availability, cash-account compliance, payment proration, no delivery failures, issuer fractional issuance or executable auction fills. Unsupported cash-dividend due bills, reverse/noninteger due-bill splits, foreign/ADR distributions, separate entitlement markets, shorts, borrow, derivatives, elections and multiple coincident actions retain refusal. Paper/live operation needs later operational controls.

## Controlling facts and calendar

Provider event identity, exact ratio and `ex_date` must agree with the independently extracted issuer identity/ratio/first split-adjusted session E. This agreement is mandatory. Only additional event-specific primary corroboration is optional; its absence is disclosed. Canonical engine `effective_date` is E. It does not mean legal effectiveness or distribution date.

The initial profile requires integer ratio r >= 2, after-close issuer distribution P, record date R with R <= P < E, and a nonempty provider redemption date equal to E. Equality is a deliberately restricted profile shape informed by the triggering observation, not proof of entitlement or a universal safe due-bill rule. A nonmatching shape requires another reviewed profile; never relax this guard in place to obtain a pass. Preserve the provider's full original normalized record and all raw occurrences.

Validate ratios exactly using Fraction of bounded positive Decimal strings, with no float conversion. Equivalent non-unit rates such as20/2 may represent10/1; equality is exact new/old ratio equality. Preserve original field strings and issuer strings rather than rewriting the provider record. A slash expression within one numeric string is not a decimal and refuses. Canonical engine rates come from the corroborated issuer annotation.

A pinned independent exchange schedule covering the entire acquired interval must equal both provider calendar dates and admitted raw-bar session dates. R, P and E retain their separate meanings; P and E must be represented sessions, with E immediately following P in that independent schedule. Retain the exchange source bytes, attributed extraction, timezone, interval, early-close metadata and actual review. A published schedule is not proof of no emergency closure or symbol halt. The independently inspected Nasdaq2024 schedule is suitable for the pilot if its exact retained bytes and extracted dates pass those comparisons. Its June7→June10 adjacency does not depend on an omitted provider session.

Issuer statements must establish pre-E seller treatment; purchaser entitlement may be explicitly inferred from those event terms and applicable due-bill rules. Source locators, direct-versus-inferred classification, producer identity and aware observation/review times are mandatory. Preserve expected/scheduled trading language rather than calling it observed broker availability. Generic rule text alone cannot establish this event's identity, ratio or timing.

At E open execute the target formed at the previous completed close, after converting existing holdings once. The target is a long/cash state, not an old-unit share order. Rebase older signal closes only when the split becomes effective, for subsequent completed-close signal formation. Do not recompute an E-open signal using E's close. Fable's suggestion to evaluate a signal at E open is not adopted: it could blur the existing temporal contract. Preserve future-prefix invariance.

## Source discrepancies and semantic review

Use one unresolved-discrepancy enum: `unresolved_ancillary_date_discrepancy`. Compare provider record/payable dates to issuer record/distribution dates without equating their meanings. Missing/null/empty metadata is retained explicitly. A difference is not described as reconciled, a typo or an operational convention unless a retained source establishes that explanation.

Redemption also needs a separate source-bound disposition. Its equality with E is a profile guard; generic schema definitions do not establish actual redemption, a vendor convention or settlement of ex-date trades. Do not accept Opus's unsupported generalization that redemption normally follows settlement of ex-date trades as a new fact.

Reject disagreement affecting identity, ratio, E, entitlement transfer, additional claims/cash or the market represented by the bars. A bare assertion that an input is unused is insufficient.
