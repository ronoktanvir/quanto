## Statistical and restore qualifications

Review the complete estimand, threshold, horizon, uncertainty/calibration method and campaign error ledger before candidate freeze. No universal252-session or Newey–West recipe is adopted by D008. Fable recommends powering at the worthwhile-effect threshold; that is not accepted as written for the evaluation adviser's proposed null `mean excess return <= delta`. Power for that null must be evaluated at a declared alternative strictly above delta; at its boundary, rejection probability is approximately the test size, not80%. The final sample plan needs a separate explicit statistical review. Model-prior/search uncertainty is not removed by a dependence adjustment.

Before unattended research scheduling, choose and verify a checkpoint on a device/account outside this Mac, its authenticated identity, backup cadence and restore procedure. Preserve a checkpoint after irreversible dispatch/consumption boundaries before scheduling further such effects, or fail closed while the external checkpoint is stale. Test rollback detection without resetting budget, exposure or dispatch entitlements. Same-disk backups are useful recovery copies but not external rollback evidence. The exact destination remains an implementation/owner configuration gate; no external upload has been performed.

An awake, signed-in Mac can run work without continuous human input; it cannot promise24/7 availability through sleep/shutdown. Opus's broader statement that such a host prevents any unattended claim is therefore qualified: claim only the duration actually observed, with outages retained.
