## Finite experiment

The first campaign uses exactly one mechanically selected instrument, at most three supported long/cash SMA specifications, and exactly three fixed per-fill cost assumptions including a zero-cost diagnostic and two nonzero cases. Exact choices remain in a private manifest. Nine candidate traces are the maximum; matched benchmarks and block reductions are separately identified, not independent discoveries. No alternative instrument, window, feed or parameter is substituted after refusal or an unfavorable result.

Freeze the roster, rationale/provenance, full acquisition envelope, common scoring dates, chronological block bounds, cost cases, primary selection cost, metrics, code and dependencies before the new acquisition. All variants have enough warmup for the longest lookback and identical scored dates. Run continuously through the three blocks without resetting cash, positions or claims. The later assessment block is **development C**, not a protected holdout. Internal identifiers `development`, `validation` and `assessment` describe roles within exposed historical screening; user-facing labels must retain their development-only scope.

Select at most one candidate using only the primary nonzero cost's validation block: positive candidate growth, greater growth than the matched benchmark, and no greater maximum drawdown. Rank eligible candidates by exact growth excess, then fewer trades, then stable candidate identifier. Exact equality defines ties; there is no discretionary “indistinguishable” judgment or post-result tolerance. A candidate failing these conditions is not selected. Other cost cases and development C are reported but cannot change the chosen candidate. This is a descriptive selection rule, not a statistical edge test.

The hypothesis adviser preferred three distinct families. The initial implementation deliberately uses related SMA variants because that accounting exists. Volatility sizing and reversal remain explicitly deferred until their distinct financial semantics are verified. The first screen cannot establish their effectiveness.

## Council dispositions and limits

- **Private freeze evidence:** accept durable registration, exact manifest hash, code identity and acquisition/event ordering. Reject the assertion that only committing a manifest hash to Git makes a private freeze verifiable. The existing application journal and independent checks establish the cooperating-process claim; neither Git timestamps nor the journal protect against a privileged owner. Exact private strategy commitments remain local.
- **Trial accounting:** any later rule, window, cost, source or score-affecting code revision requires explicit new campaign lineage and search accounting. It cannot reopen a consumed slot. Preserve the old attempt and any superseding interpretation; do not erase a failure.
- **Cost realism:** accept explicit primary-cost selection and complete stress reporting. Do not invent an exact breakeven cost from three samples or start an unregistered search for it. Report measured signs at the three assumed charges; a breakeven calculation remains unperformed until separately specified. Do not infer actual opening execution quality.
- **Episode concentration:** trade counts and fixed chronological summaries are required now. They do not isolate causal contributions of individual exits. Top-exit attribution and a credible expected-trade/power calculation are further diagnostics, not facts to invent before observations. This limitation prevents a formal efficacy claim.
- **Exposure:** all historical-screen observations, artifacts and summaries are development-exposed. Dashboard and council receive only fixed lifecycle fields, opaque candidate aliases and approved qualifications. Native reviewers' scopes and actual observations must be recorded. A separate permission is needed before putting private market values in external model contexts.
- **Unseen route:** favor a precommitted future evaluation for a model-proposed strategy because public historical knowledge cannot be erased by local isolation. Historical process-level protection remains useful only with its narrower claim and completed reconciliation. Do not freeze a final statistical campaign before a candidate exists and its sample/power plan is reviewed; declaring an arbitrary future horizon now would not supply either requirement.
