# D011 finite strategy research campaign — proposal

Status: proposal for independent review. The owner resumed Quanto on November 4 and asked agents to use the supplied quant-theory sheet to identify strategies that survive trading costs and unseen data. No new market experiment, protected reservation, or claim of investment effectiveness follows from this document.

## Decision and useful first result

Prioritize actual strategy evaluation over finishing the separate D010 result-client interface. Preserve that implementation on its existing branch. Use the sheet as a topic index, with original sources and explicit assumptions for formulas. Build a small registered campaign around supported daily long/cash trend rules before adding new signal families. Five independently briefed perspectives and the existing signed-in Fable/Opus council review the campaign boundary. Criticism, executed quality checks, and scope review remain separate implementation roles.

The immediate deliverable is a complete, finite historical development screen with every candidate, cost scenario, failure and uncertainty visible. A subsequent independently isolated evaluation must establish what survives data not used for selection. These are distinct outcomes. Existing exposure cannot be erased by downloading again, selecting another directory, or assigning a new agent.

## Candidate and data boundaries

Freeze a small private manifest before acquisition or calculation: mechanical instrument choice, stable security identity, source/feed, full requested dates, warmup and chronological scoring windows, all strategy variants, benchmarks, cost scenarios, outcome measures, failure handling, and maximum trial count. Actual instrument choices and private strategy parameters stay outside Git. Public fixtures contain invented observations only. Existing consumed controllers, experiments and council calls cannot be replayed.

Start with the existing fixed long/cash SMA engine and a small set of predeclared slow lookbacks, each evaluated on identical scored sessions. Variant lookbacks are related tests, not independent discoveries. Include the existing buy-and-hold benchmark and a disclosed zero-interest cash reference; do not silently claim a total-return reinvesting benchmark or risk-free return. No shorting, leverage, options, new borrowing model, intraday signals or portfolio optimizer. Volatility sizing and reversal enter the later queue only after their accounting and statistical contracts are reviewed.

Use the existing registered Alpaca acquisition, calendar and raw-price/action admission paths. Credentials stay confined to the fixed read-only transport and are excluded from model/research output; existing paper credentials are not assumed to be inherently read-only. Fail on missing sessions, unknown relevant actions, ambiguous identities, unsupported distributions, pagination truncation, or inconsistent adjustment basis. Do not substitute IEX for SIP or adjusted prices for raw prices. Current membership is not a historically unbiased stock universe. A deliberately selected ETF study can answer only its selected-instrument question.

Add a distinct finite campaign layer rather than broadening or replaying the D007/D008 suite. The initial layer accepts only plain historical admissions whose supported accounting does not depend on a revised, exact-study D007 contract. Any revised split profile must remain deferred until separately bound to the new campaign; refusing it does not invalidate its old accepted study. Freeze the campaign before the new acquisition, bind the admitted dataset afterwards, and claim each manifest/candidate/cost slot before invoking the existing registered explorer. Duplicate campaign invocation refuses even after a crash. Preserve each failed slot and mark unattempted slots explicitly.

Signals use only completed bars and fills occur no earlier than the following session's open. Preserve current split/dividend semantics and terminal marking. Finite generic per-fill cost scenarios are sensitivity assumptions, not empirical estimates of spreads or impact. A claim about realistic implementability requires a separately documented execution-cost model and, ultimately, observed paper fills. Report the exact approximation and stress sensitivity.

## Measurements and selection

Produce deterministic growth, drawdown, turnover, fees, exposure, and explicitly named return/risk measures for each strategy and benchmark over the same scored interval. State the duration convention, cash-rate assumption and treatment of undefined statistics. A zero denominator yields undefined, never infinity or an invented favorable score. Reconcile initial equity, dividends, cash, claims, fills and final equity independently.

Use chronological development, validation and later historical-assessment blocks to examine stability; preserve warmup separation and same-date comparison. Calculate one continuous trace per candidate/cost pair and reduce the blocks using prior-boundary wealth, without resetting holdings, cash or dividend claims. At most three candidates and three fixed cost cases yield nine traces, plus their matched benchmarks, not 27 independent discoveries. These blocks remain development-exposed, even if a block was excluded from parameter fitting. Log every attempted candidate, failure, diagnostic variant and manual revision. Do not use the later block to select and then label it untouched. Predeclare one selection criterion and allow a no-candidate result. Favor simpler candidates when results are indistinguishable; do not search until a winner appears.

Any inferential acceptance needs its own complete estimand, worthwhile-effect threshold, dependence assumptions, sample/power plan, multiplicity accounting, and fixed stopping/release rule. A Sharpe point estimate or a block bootstrap alone cannot certify an edge. The first software increment may publish descriptive development comparisons only, with `investment_validation=false` and `protected_evaluation=false` enforced by the result schema.

## Unseen-data route

The current access registry explicitly refuses protected acquisition and retains unknown legacy exposure. Do not change those flags to obtain a result. Implement evidence-backed scope reconciliation, complete acquisition/read mediation, selector/reviewer separation and an isolated evaluator under D008 before granting any protected historical claim. A fresh store does not reset old knowledge. Models' prior public-market knowledge remains a limitation even after application-level separation.

If historical coverage cannot be reconciled, use a precommitted future interval after candidate freeze as the honest final evaluation. Its start/end, sample plan, candidate, benchmark, costs and release schedule must be fixed before observations exist; early visible paper monitoring makes the affected observations development-exposed. Future observations cannot be produced in this session. In either route, ambiguous or partial access consumes the affected evaluation; there is no automatic favorable replacement.

## Operation and acceptance

Run supervised, local and finite work only. Ordinary research requires no additional subscription or rented compute. External checkpoints remain a prerequisite for unattended research, but their outstanding custody/restore work does not block supervised development experiments. Keep model council reservations within the existing shared daily allowance and retain ambiguous charges. No live broker order authority is introduced.

Before any actual campaign, independently inspect the exact implementation, freeze the complete private manifest and existing authority, and independently prepare the financial comparator. Register intent before effects; retain invalid and interrupted attempts. Expose only approved summaries in the website and agents' contexts. Update the dashboard from observed task/run state, separately from its five-minute health captures. The experiment result must say what was tested, what failed, what remains unknown, and why. Do not claim the broader desk or unseen-data requirement is complete from a development screen.
