# D012 concrete design for council review

Status: selected proposal for outside-model criticism, not accepted implementation or operation. The original [proposal](D012_PROPOSAL.md) remains unchanged at SHA-256 `490e1061d66e52d9c7e904134dd9e7c0e71d50532c16f4875a696138ae361779`.

## One explicit development attachment

Select one bounded acceptance event in the existing control audit table, with no configuration rewrite or schema migration. Each supervised D011 process must explicitly supply the exact accepted event hash to the trusted supervisor constructor. The event alone is not automatically adopted by every opener. No standalone filesystem receipt confers authority; a receipt identifies and corroborates the committed event.

Only the original device comparison receives an exception. The original root path, inode, ownership, permissions, configuration, store/principal identity and legacy-unknown state must match. Bind actual current identities of the root, control/research files and object directory. Retain the existing in-process device/inode checks. Restrict this initial repair to a nonsynthetic development store with `legacy_unknown=True`; synthetic fixture support is separate test setup, never a way to attach actual observations under invented authority.

Permit exactly one D012 acceptance event. After one exists, refuse ordinary opening without its exact explicit pin even if a device number later equals the original value. Wrong pins, duplicate/conflicting events, malformed payloads, corrupted chains or unrecognized versions refuse. No historical-device allowlist, transitive mapping, latest-timestamp preference or original-device fallback after acceptance. Another device change needs another reviewed transition; this increment does not claim general restart recovery.

## Evidence before mutation

The administrator's private frozen plan binds the selected source/interpreter, accepted policy and implementation review identities, existing root/store/principal, original configuration and schema, prior retained receipt identities, exact original/current physical identities, full current control and research table/schema fingerprints and event heads, and the unchanged prepared D011 request. Every required historical prefix must match its retained local witness. Check both SQLite integrity and complete event chains. Inspect relevant unfinished effects, holds and consumed markers without changing them. Keep the exact plan and attempt local; model packets contain only safe metadata and attributed conclusions.

The lead's observed configuration/schema/prefix/chain checks and owner-reported restart support an explicit continuity assumption under the cooperating-owner model. They do not establish external freshness, every object's present contents, a missing past volume UUID, or absence of coordinated rollback. Do not invent these assurances. Current content hashes remain checked when artifacts are used. A new volume identifier may be recorded as a future baseline only; it is not required to manufacture a past comparison.

## Exclusion and atomic acceptance

Use a bounded cross-process maintenance gate honored by the new administrative and supervised attachment paths, plus an attributed inventory/drain of already running cooperating research workers. A process list alone is insufficient. Existing callers outside the upgraded paths remain a disclosed rollout limitation; the lead must exclude their mutation during this finite operation. Do not restart unrelated website services or erase their restart history.

Acquire write-exclusion locks for the existing control and research databases in a fixed documented order, with finite lock timeouts and unchanged journal modes. Recheck the exact frozen physical identities, configuration, schema, complete table fingerprints, event heads and retained prefixes while both stores are locked. The research database is a locked witness; do not modify it. Append only the one administrative acceptance event to control and commit it atomically. This is one-database atomicity with two-store exclusion, not a distributed transaction or backup.

The bounded versioned event payload binds the unique consumed attempt, exact frozen-plan digest, original configuration/schema identities, store/principal, old/new physical identities, prior control head, research checkpoint and preserved-limit flags. It includes `legacy_unknown=True`, `external_freshness_verified=False`, `protected_evaluation=False` and `unattended_recovery=False`. The event hash authenticates its payload within the local audit chain; it is not a cryptographic proof against the owner. Avoid circular receipt/event hashing. Under the transaction, absence of any prior D012 acceptance enforces one-use uniqueness.

Verify the complete resulting chain and that every old control row and all research rows remain unchanged. Normal subsequent control growth must extend the acceptance event; an old head is not required to remain the latest head. Do not accept a bare event-kind match or an event detached from the exact chain/configuration it describes.

## Process and crash contract

Prepare a small explicit administrator entrypoint and its one-shot controller. It has no market, model, order or purchase route. The ordinary D011 helper is not authority to run it. Reuse reviewed process-supervision patterns where practical: an exclusive durable attempt before mutation, a parent-granted launch gate, selected source/Python, fresh bytecode path, finite child deadline, bounded private output and honest cleanup status. Local preflight/persistence I/O limitations must remain explicit. Never monkeypatch filesystem identities.

Loss of terminal output, timeout or failed receipt publication leaves the attempt consumed and uncertain. A separate read-only inspector may establish whether the exact complete event committed and whether old state remains intact. It cannot dispatch the administrator or automatically retry a missing event. Unresolved children or inconsistent state block continuation. The fresh-process attachment acceptance is observed separately from the write's receipt.

A fresh interpreter must reopen the original authority with the exact event pin using real filesystem calls, preserve every old exposure, reservation, invalidation, revocation, hold, pending effect and consumed-run record, and continue to refuse protected acquisition. Add only the metadata required to carry the explicit pin through the supervised D011 entrypoint; it grants no new model-facing tool. No API/model account credential is needed for repair.

## Adviser dispositions and acceptance

Five independently briefed perspectives were completed before this selection: [identity](reviews/D012-identity.md), [integrity](reviews/D012-integrity.md), [operations](reviews/D012-operations.md), [quality plan](reviews/D012-quality-plan.md) and [scope](reviews/D012-scope.md). Their production facts are explicitly lead/owner-attributed; none inspected actual data or executed the repair.

Accept their common requirements for explicit authority, immutable prior application records at the application level, bounded exclusion, exact rechecks, uncertainty retention, fresh-process verification and no external-freshness claim. Do not describe SQLite as immutable against its owner. Accept the integrity adviser's ban on old-device fallback. Choose the existing audit event over that adviser's preferred extra mapping table: a single transaction-protected event supplies the one accepted representation, avoiding a schema extension and row/event disagreement. Independent tests must establish that this choice actually enforces uniqueness and exact payload/chain binding.

Before adoption, obtain actual signed-in Fable and Opus criticism on this complete design plus attributed summaries, using the existing bounded daily model allowance and preserving uncertain holds. Their response is not implementation acceptance. Exact-source criticism, independent executed failure/quality checks and separate scope review follow implementation. Tests must cover actual competing processes, stale plans, old-row alteration, wrong/non-device identity, missing authority, repeated attempts, conflicting events, crash before/after commit, lost receipts, unchanged restrictions and fresh-process reopening without metadata monkeypatches.

After successful reattachment and verification, return to the same prepared D011 request. Register it under final source before any acquisition. This repair neither selects a strategy nor completes the empirical screen, unseen evaluation, external recovery, unattended operation or live-execution requirements. No additional owner confirmation is required for this already-authorized build repair; the owner has supplied the restart fact, and all further technical acceptance belongs to the stated evidence gates.
