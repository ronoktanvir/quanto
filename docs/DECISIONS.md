### Frozen pilot and accounting policy

Primary development pilot: NVDA raw daily SIP/USD with symbol remapping disabled. Acquire 2024-05-01 through 2024-07-12; score 2024-06-03 through 2024-07-12 using exactly the 20 immediately preceding provider-calendar sessions. Earlier acquired rows are explicitly outside calculation but remain stored and development-exposed. Refuse insufficient or inconsistent sessions instead of shifting dates. The initial plan and its signal-clarification revision are preserved with hashes and submission records before new market acquisition.

The SMA rule is a level-based state rule, not a requirement for two consecutive crossing observations. At completed session t, compare the close with the mean of the current and previous 19 closes, rebased only for splits effective by t. Above means long, below means cash, equality preserves current holdings, and initial holdings are zero. A start-above-average state can enter at the first scored open. Use starting cash 10,000 and explicit 0/10/25 bps scenarios without tuning; these are illustrative accounting scenarios, not calibrated execution costs or a recommended strategy. Buy quantity is cash divided by raw open times one plus the fee rate; a sale credits gross proceeds less the fee.

Use exact rational signal comparisons over the decimal representations of validated normalized price inputs and split ratios. Portfolio amounts and quantities use a fixed local Decimal context of precision 34 with ROUND_HALF_EVEN. Preserve decimal source rates and report exact decimal strings; document any prior price normalization separately rather than implying source precision was invented. No cent rounding, cash-in-lieu or actual broker fractional-share processing is claimed. Loaded code identity must include the new engine helpers and the actual numerical policy.

Per session: release previously earned dividends with payable date strictly before the session; apply effective splits to holdings; accrue current ex-date dividends from pre-trade shares; execute the prior completed-session target at the raw open; release dividends payable on this session at its modeled end; mark raw close plus cash and outstanding claims; then form the next target. End-of-pay-date cash is not usable at that day's open. Same-session ambiguous split/dividend combinations reject in this increment, even though the supported operation order is explicit. Synthetic tests must distinguish this refusal from supported payment/split/trade combinations.

Dividend rates must be established per share outstanding at the ex-date boundary. Admission preserves provider and issuer rates, the stated share basis, and any attributed conversion judgment. Unresolved basis rejects; price movements cannot establish it. A later split never multiplies an accrued nominal claim. Initially cash portfolios inherit no warmup dividends. No automatic reinvestment occurs while holding; cash may fund a later ordinary entry. The benchmark buys once at the same first eligible open, retains split-adjusted quantities and cash dividends, and never redeploys dividends. Both end marked at the same final close, include unpaid claims at nominal value and incur no terminal liquidation or invented exit fee. Report initial/ending equity, cash, quantity, receivables, trades, fees and event flows.
