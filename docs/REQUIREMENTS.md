No item is complete merely because a document or test name exists. Inspect implementation, executed checks, and operational evidence appropriate to its scope.

Completion does not mean guaranteed profitability. It means the requested operation exists, runs, and passes its declared integrity and operational criteria. An available but unactivated live service must be distinguished from a live operation; owner intent determines the final activation gate.
