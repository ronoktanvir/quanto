## Objections and dispositions recommended to the lead

- **Deferring all exposure records creates irreversible ambiguity.** Accept deferral of the final access-control mechanism only with development-only pilot coverage, basic exploratory exposure records and an explicit next milestone. A new hash must never restore an exposed interval.
- **A strict admission gate can hide a stalled product.** Keep unknown action/timing conditions as financial blockers, but require the dashboard and saved artifact to identify the missing source or accounting capability and next action. Do not report R10 complete merely because unsupported studies are rejected.
- **Source rights must constrain a specific use.** Retain the source adviser's Coinbase/FRED objections and pending Alpaca agreement inspection. Avoid converting unverified redistribution or external-model rights into an unexplained blanket stop on unrelated local implementation. A rights state records evidence and permitted use; it is not itself permission.
- **US-first must not erase crypto or broader warehouse scope.** A few current US symbols are a pilot. Delisted coverage, corporate actions, point-in-time fundamentals, multi-asset semantics and eventual crypto source selection remain explicit future work. Do not imply that a generic manifest schema completes any of them.
- **Financial validity does not require a profitable demo.** A faithfully recorded rejected study advances the workflow. It does not establish investment performance, and it does not discharge the need to run an eligible study once the data and accounting dependencies are resolved.
