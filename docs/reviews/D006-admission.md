# D006 independent adviser: research admission and information integrity

**Recommendation: admit a bounded, explicitly retrospective historical calculation after implementing verifiable input checks and supported corporate-action accounting. Do not require proof that a vendor database is complete or point-in-time before allowing any real-data calculation.** That proof is unavailable from the inspected interfaces. Its absence restricts the inference that follows from a calculation; it does not make a calculation on preserved observations impossible. Conversely, calling an output “exploratory” does not excuse known accounting errors, unsupported events, missing sessions, or an unexamined permission boundary.

This is a proposal review, not implementation certification, independent quality validation, a data entitlement determination, or an investment recommendation. ## What is established, and what is missing

The documented pilot acquired raw bars, adjusted bars, and calendar observations with 21 matching session dates. Every raw/adjusted price row differed. That supports proceeding to investigate adjustment semantics; it does not establish an action inside the pilot interval. An adjustment resulting from an event outside the interval can also alter older prices. Neither differences nor equality identify a complete event history.

The implementation establishes a useful provenance boundary. `explorer.py` verifies stored bytes, records development exposure, freezes experiment identity, and refuses historical studies before calculation. `research.py` accepts the historical evidence string at its pure function boundary, but rejects every nonempty corporate-action list and every non-raw price basis. It never credits dividends or adjusts share quantities for splits. Its default empty list cannot constitute an absence-of-actions finding. The registered workflow's refusal therefore prevents a genuine unsupported-accounting path; removing only that refusal would not implement admission.

The intended first result should answer a limited question: **What path does this fixed price rule produce on these preserved current vendor revisions, for these declared instruments and dates, under this explicit accounting and execution scenario?** It must not answer whether the rule was historically deployable, selected without hindsight, profitable after actual costs, or validated out of sample.

## Evidence standard and decision boundary

Admission should be a versioned, reproducible decision over referenced artifacts, not a mutable `approved: true` field. Its scope should include the instrument episode, requested and evaluated dates, warmup, exact bar/calendar/action bytes, normalizer and engine identities, accounting policy, cost assumptions, source-use disposition, and unresolved limitations. A result must retain the exact admission record and be re-admitted when a dependency changes.

Require enough preserved evidence to make the declared episode coherent, and confine the result to it. A current asset identifier alone does not prove its historical continuity. New symbols or alternative identifiers must not erase prior exposure; unresolved aliases preclude claims of unexposed data even when exploratory calculations remain possible.

## Corporate-action coverage without fictitious completeness

The present corporate-actions API documents a significant trap: its default quality filter can omit incomplete, unprocessed entries. It offers an all-quality mode, lists event types beyond dividends and splits, paginates, and describes its date bounds in terms of processing dates. It also gives no guarantee of when records become available. Those properties require explicit collection and uncertainty handling. [Alpaca corporate-actions reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1)

The proposed acquisition must request all available action categories and include incomplete records for detection. Selecting only the categories the engine supports hides the very events that should reject admission. An incomplete event may be useful adverse evidence even though it is unusable for accounting. Preserve provider event identifiers, original payloads, retrieval time, effective/ex dates, payable dates, process dates, and a distinct “historical availability unknown” field; never substitute process date for economic effect or knowledge time.

Do not query only the backtest dates and assume the response covers every economically relevant event. Use a documented bounded collection policy that spans available historical processing records through the retrieval date for the small selected instrument set, or justify another sufficiently broad scope with provider evidence. Filter locally by economic relevance, retaining later payments on entitlements earned during the evaluated interval. Include warmup events and instrument changes. Bound pages, bytes and elapsed time; exhausted bounds are failure, not empty coverage. A finite extra lag does not eliminate an unbounded publication delay. If the supported history is shorter than the study, narrow the declared study prospectively or obtain another authorized source; record the rejected attempt.

The attainable no-observed-event condition is: a successful preserved query under that policy, completed pagination, validated fields, coherent identities and date coverage, no detected relevant unsupported event, and no unresolved contradictory evidence. It means **no relevant event observed in these sources as retrieved**, not “no event occurred.” An empty caller list, empty failed query, default quality-filtered response, or equal raw/adjusted prices cannot satisfy this condition. Residual omissions can be accepted as a stated limitation of this development calculation once these checks exist. Otherwise the proposed relaxation is a label-only bypass.

Treat raw/adjusted comparisons as diagnostics. Alpaca documents `all` as including splits, cash dividends and spin-offs; `asof=-` disables automatic name mapping. Consequently, adjusted bars are not automatically tradable prices or a universal total-return solution. [Alpaca historical-bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) A mismatch may identify an expected factor or a question requiring reconciliation; it need not block merely because prices differ. An unexplained factor change or conflict with known actions should block the affected study. Equal prices never override a positive action record.

## Minimum accounting worth admitting

Use raw prices for hypothetical transaction values and marks. Add a typed event ledger and independently checked accounting for a deliberately narrow set, preferably ordinary cash dividends and simple forward/reverse splits. Everything else must have an explicit refusal path, including spin-offs, mergers, rights, redemptions, stock dividends, uncertain currencies, special distributions, ambiguous effective times, and combinations whose event ordering is not implemented. An ETF label does not exempt its distributions from this rule. If a proposed initial implementation supports fewer events, state that limitation and preserve a concrete expansion milestone.

For a split, transform the existing share quantity before the effective session's transaction processing. Normalize only the signal lookback to the share basis at that signal date; do not feed permanently future-adjusted prices into raw portfolio accounting. A split becoming effective tomorrow must not change yesterday's signal. Quantities, raw prices, fees and benchmark treatment must remain coherent. Fractional quantities may be an explicit theoretical assumption, but fractional entitlement and cash-in-lieu behavior must not be described as broker-supported execution.

For ordinary cash dividends, determine entitlement from the position before the ex-date, recognize a separate receivable in equity, and transfer it to cash under a stated payable-date convention. Do not count the receivable twice or reinvest it before cash is available under that convention. Retain unpaid receivables at the end of the scored period, or explicitly report a different valuation scope. Purchases on the ex-date do not acquire the ordinary entitlement; selling on that date does not remove the existing entitlement. Special dividend and stock-distribution rules differ and should be refused until supported. [SEC Investor.gov dividend explanation](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/ex-dividend-dates-when-are-you-entitled-stock-and)

Specify whether ordinary dividend price drops remain part of the price signal. A raw-price signal normalized for splits only is a coherent initial choice; silently adding dividend adjustments changes the strategy. Both portfolios need identical economic accounting and common scored dates, even though their holdings and resulting entitlements differ. Known events cannot be discarded because the strategy happens to be in cash: the benchmark or warmup signal can still depend on them.

## Historical timing and execution claims

Two distinct uses of action information need separate treatment. Accounting may reconstruct an economic event at its effective date using a record obtained today. A strategy may not claim it anticipated that event using today's record. The proposed price-only rule should receive no announcement fields and make no event-driven decision. The retrospective signal can use effect-date split normalization with its limitations disclosed; a prospective system must establish what was actually received and when.

Next-session ordering removes direct same-close execution, but does not make latest downloaded bars point-in-time. A completed close retrieved today may differ from what was then available. There is no general lag constant that repairs that unknown revision history. Admit it as a current-revision retrospective calculation and reserve historical deployability claims for suitable archived observations or prospective evidence.

Alpaca documents daily bars as aggregates governed by trade-condition rules, with New York day labels rather than availability instants. Its stock stream separately documents corrections to previously reported trades. These facts support treating raw bars as observations rather than execution guarantees. [Aggregation rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq), [stock-stream corrections](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data)

Describe an open-based fill as an assumed next-session reference-price transaction. Do not call it an auction execution, an attainable price, or a demonstrated liquidity model. Require cost inputs instead of inheriting the current zero-cost default accidentally. A zero-cost diagnostic is permissible when explicit, accompanied by preregistered nonzero sensitivity scenarios. Illustrative cost values are scenarios, not empirical estimates. Report settlement, fractional-share, capacity and available-cash simplifications; they can qualify a theoretical calculation but cannot satisfy paper/live operational readiness.

## Adversarial objections and required tests

These are proposed acceptance tests, not executed results. Independent numerical validation should use hand-derived synthetic fixtures; a separate private historical run must demonstrate that the real admission path works without exposing source values externally.

1. **The label could bypass the gate.** Supply forged approval labels, alternate evidence labels, a bare empty event list, or an admission record referencing different bytes. Reject without running the engine; preserve the attempt and exposure. Admission must recompute verifiable conditions and bind all dependency hashes.
2. **All three price/calendar sources could agree yet miss an action.** Inject a valid split event despite equal raw/adjusted bars; accounting must apply it or reject the contradiction. Empty, unavailable, truncated, malformed, incomplete and unsupported action responses must be distinguishable. Neither equality nor an empty list may produce an “action-free verified” status.
3. **Filtering could conceal the blocker.** Include a spin-off or name change alongside supported dividends; include an incomplete event whose relevant interval cannot be determined. Reject the affected instrument/window rather than silently dropping the record. Include an event processed outside the economic study window and confirm collection/relevance logic catches it.
4. **Splits could create false gains or signals.** Hand-check a pure 2-for-1 split: quantity doubles, raw price halves, marked value is conserved absent market movement and costs. Test reverse splits, warmup boundaries, pending entry/exit, multiple events, unsupported fractional treatment and both portfolios. Change a future event and assert earlier signals/trades are unchanged.
5. **Dividends could become spendable too soon.** Test purchase before/on ex-date, sale on ex-date, payout after exit, non-session payment dates, pending terminal receivables and repeated provider event IDs. A buyer on ex-date receives nothing; the receivable cannot fund an earlier buy; cash plus receivables reconciles without duplicate income. Unknown or special entitlement rules reject.
6. **Calendar checks could be tautological.** Remove one bar and then supply its shortened date list. Reject against the independently preserved calendar artifact, not a list derived from bars. Exercise holiday, early close, daylight-saving, extra bar and missing warmup cases.
7. **Revisions could masquerade as new unseen evidence.** Change bar or action bytes under the same symbol/range. Require a new artifact/admission/result identity, preserve old outcomes and mark overlapping development exposure. Record disagreements and superseding results without erasing previously reported calculations. Renaming studies cannot clear exposure.
8. **Costs and benchmark differences could manufacture an edge.** Hand-check fees on each fill, common starting cash and common scoring dates, no implicit terminal sale, dividend receipts and split treatment. Verify deterministic replay for frozen scenarios and reject nonfinite/unsupported parameters. Do not assert general strategy-return monotonicity under changing costs when altered cash paths can change subsequent quantities.
9. **Timing disclaimers could hide actual leakage.** Mutate future bars, future-effective actions and announcement metadata. No earlier signal may change. Force a completed-close signal and assert its earliest fill is the next eligible session. Preserve the latest-revision limitation even when these tests pass.
10. **Failure or revision could erase criticism.** Corrupt an action/calendar object after admission; interrupt publication between report and terminal outcome; re-fetch after disagreement. Fail closed, retain pending/failed records, and keep prior review objections linked to their original artifact identities.

## Completion and unresolved gates

D006 should be accepted only after a concrete policy is implemented, independent criticism and numerical validation inspect exact revisions, and at least one authorized private historical study traverses the complete admission path. Report that outcome as a historical, development-only retrospective calculation with its supported events and limitations. Do not advertise “validated data,” “edge,” “unseen holdout,” or strategy readiness merely because a run succeeded.

Protected final-evaluation access is still required. Current datasets are development-exposed; later specification changes, fresh downloads or a different model do not restore them. Final evaluation needs a separate reviewed access/exposure policy and an appropriately protected interval or prospective design. The five operational research roles, searchable failure memory, reliable scheduling, prospective paper operation, separately credentialed execution and explicit owner live activation also remain required. Crypto is a subsequent separately reviewed source/accounting scope. These future gates should not keep the operation permanently synthetic, and this increment must not be presented as completing them.
