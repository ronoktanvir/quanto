# D006 independent design advice — full scope and attainable sequencing

Author: Codex scope adviser, independently briefed as `/root/d006_scope_adviser` by the lead. Prepared 2026-11-02 UTC. This is one design perspective. It is not the subsequent independent no-drift certification, implementation criticism, quality validation, outside-model review, or five-perspective consensus. I wrote only this report, ran no tests, and did not inspect `.env`, runtime records or private market payloads, or call provider, broker or paid-model endpoints. Reported pilot outcomes below come from project documentation, not independent reproduction.

## Decision

Proceed with a bounded raw-price event engine and evidence-backed **current-revision retrospective research mode**. D006 should end with an actual registered historical calculation exercising ordinary cash-dividend and split accounting, reproducible from preserved bytes, with exact assumptions and independent implementation review. It should neither stop at another inspection receipt nor require a complete point-in-time warehouse before any useful real-data calculation can occur.

The scope distinction is decisive: qualified descriptive calculation is attainable now; strategy validation and operation readiness remain later work. Accepting documented residual vendor omissions and historical revision uncertainty for development does not justify ignoring a detected unsupported event, contradictory evidence, missing required event terms, or unverified input identity. Those conditions still refuse the affected calculation.

## What this increment must accomplish

The baseline already records real Alpaca acquisition and development exposure, but the registered workflow refuses historical calculation. The research core has no dividend receivable or split-quantity accounting. D006 therefore needs a working path through acquisition, normalization, scoped coverage assessment, admission, registered experiment, portfolio accounting, benchmark and durable result. Changing the refusal label or adding a caller-authored approval field would not close that gap.

A concrete pilot should use a finite declared instrument episode, a fixed price-only SMA specification, explicit warmup and common evaluation dates, and a small frozen set of cost scenarios. Retain zero-cost output only as a named diagnostic beside nonzero scenarios; the scenario values are assumptions, not measured execution costs. Do not tune the specification after inspecting results and then present it as preregistered. Record every rejected, invalid and failed attempt, including a decision to change the pilot window or instrument.

Prefer one source-supported interval that contains a simple split and an ordinary cash dividend, rather than a succession of event-free windows. The accounting adviser identifies NVIDIA's June 2024 split/dividend documentation as a candidate normalization reference; the lead should freeze exact dates, sufficient warmup and identity evidence before acquiring the pilot. If that combined interval cannot be supported, an ordinary-dividend AAPL interval plus a separately bounded known split interval is an acceptable alternative. These are proposed pilot choices, not certifications of event eligibility or acquired prices. A failed source/admission attempt must remain visible; do not quietly search until a convenient empty action response appears.

The completed private pilot needs all of the following evidence:

1. Preserved raw bars, provider calendar, all-category action acquisition and relevant primary-source corroboration, with exact requests, receipts, source-use disposition, identities and bytes. A bounded action process-date envelope is distinct from economic study dates and the dividend payment tail; exhausted pagination is incomplete acquisition.
2. An admission record bound to those artifacts and software identities, stating selected instrument episode, coverage assessment, unsupported-event checks, current-revision timing, currency, signal basis, cash availability, fees and remaining uncertainties. Hashes establish identity, not truth or permission.
3. A registered historical study whose actual evidence kind remains `historical_market_data`, with same-window strategy and benchmark results, split applications, dividend receivables/payments, cash, quantities and final equity reconciled. Exact private result identities may be recorded publicly inside this private repository without publishing source values.
4. Deterministic replay from the frozen local artifacts, hand-derived accounting fixtures and adversarial refusal checks, then separate criticism, quality and scope reviews identifying their inspected revision and artifacts. Fixtures verify software; the private historical run verifies that the real admission path works. Neither establishes an investment edge.
5. Updated STATUS, HANDOFF, REQUIREMENTS and decision dispositions that distinguish implemented capability, actual executed evidence, limitations and unfinished operation requirements. Passing design review alone does not satisfy these acceptance criteria.

If source access or required terms are actually unavailable, implement and independently verify the reversible engine/admission work, report the exact remaining dependency, and continue independent operational components. D006's real-data acceptance remains incomplete until a historical run meets the criteria. A pending dependency should not become an indefinite synthetic-only definition of success.

## Boundaries that preserve honest scope

**Identity needs a supported episode, not a premature universal security master.** Freeze provider ticker, mapping policy, share class, relevant identifier evidence, interval continuity and selection rationale. Refuse known ticker reuse or unresolved predecessor/successor joins. Restrict the first result to that selected episode; it cannot represent an unbiased historical universe. Keep a durable alias relationship when established so a rename or fresh download cannot erase exposure. A fully general point-in-time universe and delisted coverage remain future requirements when the research question requires them.

**Source-use review is factual work, not a repeated owner-permission gate.** The owner already authorized this private Alpaca pilot. The action-source adviser distinguishes general personal-use terms from uninspected account/exchange agreements and finds no basis to treat a failed public PDF fetch as a new requirement that the owner reapprove the same task. Record what was actually reviewed, the intended private use, outstanding account-specific uncertainty and any material restriction. Resolve a concrete restriction or uncertainty that affects the proposed use before that use; do not demand publication or redistribution rights for a local development calculation. No subscription purchase or unauthorized source substitution follows from access failure.

**Historical values remain outside external-model packets.** Existing authorization for outside-model criticism does not authorize transmitting licensed bars, normalized market values, data-bearing result tables, private strategies or credentials. Use sanitized architecture, synthetic examples, code and non-data-bearing evidence metadata for the required actual Opus review. Keep any necessary review of private numerical results local, with named artifacts and provenance. If a model packet cannot be safely constructed, report that particular review pending and continue authorized reversible work; never record a simulated critique as actual outside-model evidence.

**The benchmark tests a comparison, not investability.** A same-start buy-and-hold portfolio with cash dividends retained must share raw prices, fees, split accounting, payment policy and scored dates with the SMA portfolio. Include cash as a simple reference where useful. Do not call this a reinvested total-return index or use a mismatched provider-adjusted benchmark to infer an edge. Daily opens are hypothetical reference-price fills; settlement, broker cash credits, fractional processing and liquidity remain explicit simplifications. Benchmark parity is necessary for interpretation and insufficient for execution readiness.

**Five advisers are not the five research workers.** This panel informs a significant design decision. It does not execute the persistent director, hypothesis researcher, experiment engineer, data/implementation critic and statistical/portfolio critic roles required by R14. Contextual desk conversations also do not satisfy those roles. The later operation needs bounded authority, actual role outputs tied to run artifacts, preserved objections and decisions, and separate independent criticism, quality validation and no-drift review. A producer cannot certify its own work by changing its role label.

## Sequence after D006

| Milestone | Concrete evidence needed | Why it follows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protected evaluation and searchable memory | Frozen specifications, audited access/exposure policy, exact overlap/alias handling, retrieval of prior failed/rejected attempts and their dispositions | Development runs may inform a hypothesis, but changed code or new downloads do not restore an exposed holdout. |
| Actual bounded five-role research cycle | One real-data question carried through all five workers, deterministic experiment output and distinct review dispositions | This turns a calculation tool into the requested research operation; adviser reports and dashboard chats cannot substitute. |
| Durable scheduled research | Persisted bounded job identity, leases/fencing, interruption reconciliation, controlled retries, budget enforcement, restart/restore checks and observed unattended runs | Website uptime and five-minute status capture do not demonstrate research scheduling. |
| Prospective paper operation | Timestamped observations and decisions, supported fill/cash assumptions, position reconciliation, recovery and a declared observation period | Prospective evidence addresses historical revision and deployability limitations rather than concealing them in a retrospective disclaimer. |
| Separately credentialed execution readiness | Isolated broker adapter, limits, emergency stop, reconciliation, concrete reviewed strategy and explicit owner activation | Research authorization never authorizes live orders. Owner activation is a genuine final gate, not a reason to stop independent construction. |
| Crypto and broader coverage | Separate source-use, identity, calendar, event/funding and accounting decisions with real evidence | US-first sequencing preserves the crypto objective without pretending equity assumptions transfer automatically. |

These milestones can overlap where dependencies permit. A complete multi-market warehouse, public release and a profitable strategy are not prerequisites to the first coherent research cycle. They are also not established by it. Keep R10–R15 and R17–R19 incomplete to the extent their declared evidence remains absent; no percentage or milestone label should convert partial foundations into the full operation.

## Dissent and disposition requested

I support the accounting/admission/source/operations direction with two corrections to possible interpretations. First, “manual pilot” should mean a deliberately invoked bounded job, which the authorized lead can run; it must not impose a new requirement that the owner type every command or remain present. Second, independent source corroboration and residual coverage assessment should permit a specifically qualified development result; they must not silently escalate into a demand for universal proof of action absence. Conversely, if an action conflict is known, describing vendor incompleteness in a disclaimer cannot override it.

The lead should record whether these objections are accepted, modified or rejected. The meaningful D006 outcome is the first reproducible and financially coherent real-data research calculation under declared limitations, followed by continued construction of the full operation. No investment recommendation, profitability claim or live-promotion conclusion follows from this report.
