## Assessment of the proposal

The core design is sound and internally consistent with the cited public mechanics: a regular-way old-unit share is one economic package including the attached split interest; one conversion `q → rq` at the first adjusted open E, with lookback closes rebased from E, correctly represents pre-E sellers (no orphan claim), post-record/pre-E buyers (entitlement acquired), and E-open buyers (no extra award). FINRA 11140(b)(2) (≥25% distributions ex on the first business day after payable) is consistent with issuer dates R=Jun 6, P=Jun 7 after close, E=Jun 10, and the OCC memo corroborates ratio and dates. Keeping the engine unchanged and placing the change in admission/policy identity is the right locus. The post-acquisition framing, retained D006 refusal, and `investment_evidence=false` labeling are correct.

The following are the consequential problems.

## Blockers (must be resolved before admission)

**B1. No independent semantic review of the actual event annotation is an admission precondition.** The proposal gates re-admission on "affected-code criticism and quality checks," which are software reviews. The `regular_way_split` object is an attributed human judgment (entitlement inference, discrepancy dispositions, locators). D007-sources and D007-integrity both require a separate reviewer to inspect the actual judgment and locators before admission; the proposal does not list this step. Correction: add an explicit gate—"annotation author ≠ implementer/lead where feasible; independent reviewer records identity, timestamp and outcome against the annotation hash before the admission request is submitted." Without it the only semantic check is self-certification.

**B2. Provider ex-date requirement is ambiguous.** "Primary issuer terms govern them, with provider ex-date/ratio/identity agreement and a second event-specific primary corroboration where available" can be read as making provider ex-date agreement optional. D007-integrity treats `ex_date == E` as hard. Correction: state unambiguously that provider `ex_date`, ratio direction/value and security identity must equal the issuer-derived E/ratio/identity (hard refuse on mismatch); only the second primary corroboration is "where available."

**B3. Three-scenario execution vs. single-scenario registration is contradictory.** "Run the three frozen scenarios" and "registered studies … choose exactly one of the predeclared 0/10/25 bps scenarios" are not reconciled. This matters for fingerprint, replay-equality and oracle-comparison semantics. Correction: declare either three separately registered studies (one per scenario, shared admission and plan revision) or one study with three predeclared scenario outputs, and fix the fingerprint rule accordingly before any run.

**B4. Oracle independence is unaddressed.** The "preserved independent rational oracle" was presumably built under D006. The proposal must state whether the oracle consumes the canonical engine event directly (no shared admission/normalization code) and whether it needs any change. If the oracle is modified to accept the new event, that modification needs its own identity and review; otherwise the "identical replay/oracle agreement" success criterion is weakened.

## Ambiguities / corrections

**C1. Pilot may be unadmittable under this profile, by design.** The Alpaca schema example places redemption one day after ex. If the actual retained NVDA record has `due_bill_redemption_date ≠ E`, the "redemption == E" guard refuses and the pilot stalls. The decision should pre-state the fallback: a new reviewed profile and policy version, never loosening the guard in place after seeing the field. Currently implied but not explicit.

**C2. Buyer-side entitlement is inference, not issuer statement.** The issuer FAQ addresses sellers through June 7; the post-record/pre-E buyer's entitlement derives from 11630 due-bill mechanics plus the seller statement. This is the weakest evidentiary leg. The annotation must mark it as attributed inference (the proposal permits this) and the admission summary should surface it, not bury it under "entitlement established."

**C3. Signal-rebasing order should be explicit.** State that lookback closes are divided by `r` before the E-open signal is evaluated, and that no rebasing occurs for any session < E. The negative fixture "future-split metadata rebasing earlier signals" plus "constant equivalent closes produce no split-only signal" cover it, but the contract text should say it once, plainly.

**C4. Calendar authority for E.** "Next retained provider-calendar session after P" relies solely on the provider calendar. Require E to also be a session present in the admitted raw bars and, where retained, an independent exchange calendar; refuse on disagreement.

**C5. Rule-version binding.** FINRA 11140 as currently published reflects the May 28, 2024 amendment, which is correct for a June 2024 event, but the retained evidence should be the rule text with retrieval hash/date, not a URL, and the admission must not assert that this version applies to earlier events in any future generalization.

**C6. Duplicate FAQ URLs.** Accounting/sources cite `s201.q4cdn.com`; integrity cites `investor.nvidia.com`. Retain one canonical hashed copy, or both with byte-equality recorded. Differing bytes under one "FAQ" label would be a discrepancy to disclose.

**C7. Rate normalization.** Specify exact decimal-string normalization for `new_rate/old_rate` (e.g., "10" vs "10.0" vs "10/1") before equality tests against the issuer ratio; otherwise a trivial formatting difference becomes a false conflict or, worse, a silent rewrite.
