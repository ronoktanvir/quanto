## Required evidence contract

Use a versioned, strictly validated split-reconciliation object associated with each provider ID. Required contents:

1. **Exact source binding:** provider ID/category; hash of the complete original record; all page/record occurrences; source-document hashes, URLs, exact locators, retrieval receipts when available; reviewer identity and timezone-aware review timestamp. Bind the provider-schema/source-semantics version as retained evidence. A URL alone is insufficient.
2. **Issuer terms:** issuer/security identity and continuity; positive decimal-string old/new shares; record date and its boundary; legal effective timestamp if stated; distribution date and before/after-close boundary; first split-adjusted regular-session date; stated buyer/seller entitlement treatment. Each extracted fact and interpretation needs a source hash/locator. Do not collapse three dates into one field or fabricate missing timestamps.
3. **Provider comparison:** preserve the actual presence/value of `ex_date`, `record_date`, `payable_date`, `process_date`, and `due_bill_redemption_date`, including absent/null/empty distinctions. For each, record the mapped issuer fact or documented provider meaning, comparison outcome, and whether a discrepancy is explained or unresolved. Preserve symbol, identifiers, rates and currency. Unknown extra fields require explicit schema treatment, not silent removal.
4. **Scoped interpretation:** a source-linked explanation that regular-way beneficial economic holdings transfer with trading, so the transformation at first adjusted session neither leaves the pre-boundary seller an extra entitlement nor denies the pre-boundary purchaser one. State why no additional modeled cash/claim, election, alternate share class or separate traded entitlement is required. This is an attributed judgment, not a machine-proven assertion.

Mechanical validation proves retained bytes, receipt relationships, exact equality, canonical dates, rate arithmetic, session membership and complete annotation matching. It cannot prove that a passage means what a reviewer claims. Keep attribution explicit; a generic `regular_way_verified=true` or `trusted_source=true` must not replace the evidence object. Locators and narrative alone also require independent semantic review.

Alpaca distinguishes processing dates from economic dates and warns that corporate-action availability can lag announcement. Its schema describes redemption as due-bill-obligation redemption, not permission to discard the field. [Alpaca corporate-actions reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1). Accordingly, `process_date` remains provenance/coverage metadata; it never supplies either effective session or historical knowledge time.

## Allow/refuse boundary

Allow this new path only when all existing receipt, raw-price, session, identity, coverage, rights and duplicate checks pass, plus:

- Category is an ordinary forward split with `new_rate > old_rate > 0`, matching issuer terms and studied security; no unresolved conversion or accompanying entitlement.
- Provider ex-date equals the independently extracted first adjusted regular session, present in the admitted calendar. Retained evidence establishes old units through the preceding close and new units at that open. Prices cannot supply this judgment.
- Present provider record/payable fields are compared explicitly with issuer record/distribution dates. Missing optional metadata is labeled unknown. Explained differences retain their evidence; unresolved differences require the narrowly qualified authority policy below.
- A nonempty redemption date is valid and reconciled to documented due-bill semantics. For the initial narrow policy, require it to equal the first adjusted session as an additional restriction; equality alone never qualifies. Nonmatching dates need a separate reviewed expansion.
- Issuer timing and entitlement interpretation establish that the daily economic model needs exactly one transformation. No settling-position snapshots, additional due-bill receivables or broker inventory restrictions are claimed.

A fixed **economic-field authority policy** is defensible: issuer evidence governs the ratio, first adjusted session and economic entitlement; provider identity/ex-date must corroborate them. A provider record/payable disagreement may remain unresolved only if exact event identity is established and independent issuer evidence directly determines economic holdings for pre-boundary sellers, purchasers and continuing holders. Retain both dates, mark `unresolved_ancillary_date_discrepancy`, identify the exact fields and attributed rationale, and disclose that no settlement reconciliation was achieved. Do not claim differing field meanings without evidence. This exception must be an enumerated, versioned rule, not a per-event override Boolean. Reject conflicts about ratio, security, first adjusted session, purchaser/seller entitlement, extra economic claims, or evidence suggesting the record refers to another action. These change or leave uncertain the modeled position. An unexplained record/payable discrepancy alone need not do so when direct entitlement evidence closes that uncertainty. If that direct evidence is absent, refuse. This qualification should be decided and frozen before calculation, never inferred from a favorable result.

Refuse unresolved economic-boundary dates, malformed dates, non-session mapping, wrong units, unsupported/ambiguous entitlement mechanics, missing source binding, conflicting identifiers or duplicate records, unmatched annotations, and same-session unsupported combinations. Preserve existing ordinary-dividend exclusions: this exception must not admit special cash distributions, dividend due bills, reverse/unit splits, spin-offs, stock dividends, mergers, or cash-in-lieu accounting. Existing supported cases retain their existing rules.

Required negative cases include equal ex/redemption dates with missing entitlement evidence; equal dates with a contradictory issuer trading date; legal/distribution date substituted for the first adjusted open; redemption after that open; missing/malformed redemption; mismatched record/payable dates without the bounded authority evidence; altered record under the same provider ID; changed/tombstoned source bytes; unbound attribution; future-split metadata rebasing earlier signals; and a policy/plan revision mismatch. Test absent optional metadata separately from contradictory metadata, and qualified ancillary disagreements separately from economic conflicts. Synthetic tests establish these software boundaries only.

## Canonical event and historical honesty

Keep the engine event exactly `kind`, `event_id`, first-adjusted-session `effective_date`, `new_rate`, `old_rate`. Retain all legal, record, distribution, process, redemption and entitlement evidence in admission, with a hash-linked mapping from original record to canonical event. The ACCOUNTING contract already transforms holdings and earlier signal closes at this session; extending the engine to simulate settlement would expand scope unnecessarily. If the evidence requires a second economically valued entitlement or different units before the boundary, this canonical event is insufficient: refuse rather than normalize it away.

Add an explicit content-addressed plan revision and D007 policy/decision identity to the admission dependency graph. The new revision must identify its predecessor, triggering refused submissions, exact acquired dataset/receipt identities, unchanged strategy/windows/cost scenarios, reason for change, review identities, and actual creation time. Bind it through a versioned admission request/schema and into the resulting experiment fingerprint; merely attaching an unvalidated note is insufficient. Revalidate it before and after execution with existing source/loaded-code checks. Changed code/policy requires a fresh admission, not reinterpretation of the old object.

Preserve every initial/revised request, invalid outcome, interrupted/pending publication and original facts/plan. Append new artifacts and outcomes without modifying previous records; content addressing and application append-only controls do not make owner-controlled storage immutable. Reusing already-acquired exact bytes avoids unnecessary acquisition but confers no preregistration. Label the resulting run **post-acquisition policy revision, retrospective development only**, retain full acquired exposure and unknown outside exposure, and retain `investment_evidence=false`.

The information-time limit remains decisive. Current retrieved issuer/provider revisions can justify retrospective unit mapping, but neither process date nor an old document's printed date proves when this system could have known it. Log publication/revision claims separately from observed retrieval time; never invent availability. Future events must not change earlier numerical outputs, and event announcements must not become trading signals. No strategy, evaluation window, lookback or cost change is part of this decision.

The strongest objection is that a post-acquisition exception can become repeated accommodation of inconvenient data. The defense is a general, narrow predicate frozen before any portfolio calculation, retained refusal history, independent objections and exact comparisons. This permits a useful accounting pilot while preserving the fact that its policy was informed by observed data. Full D007 review, affected-code criticism and quality validation remain gates; historical performance and execution readiness remain unestablished.
