## Conditions for the exception

Admission should require issuer-primary evidence bound to the exact studied issuer/security episode that establishes the split identity, forward ratio, effective/ex-date terms, and first adjusted trading session. It must also establish the economic entitlement for ordinary regular-way trades occurring before the ex-date: which economic shares receive the post-split quantity and when that entitlement attaches. The engine may then apply the ratio once to economic holdings before ex-open trades, and must not use a vendor ancillary date or due-bill label to shift that operation. An equality between vendor dates is not evidence of entitlement.

The exception should be limited to issuer-identified common stock and ordinary forward splits. Exclude broker settlement or delivery ledgers, when-issued trading, short positions, stock loan/borrow, non-common or multi-asset distributions, discretionary or unresolved due bills, and any event whose relevant identity, ratio, ex/effective session, first adjusted session, or entitlement cannot be reconciled. Preserve every source occurrence, raw ancillary field, conflicting annotation, and attributed extraction judgment. Ancillary record/payable date conflicts can remain unresolved only where source evidence shows those dates do not determine the split's economic entitlement, share conversion, or modeled cash flow. Do not silently override them or collapse differing sources into a single asserted fact.
