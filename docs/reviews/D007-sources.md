**Recommendation: conditionally support an event-specific regular-way economic-share transformation, while retaining D006's refusal until the new policy and the actual source reconciliation pass review.** A populated due-bill field does not by itself establish an economically separate distribution; equal ex/redemption dates do not establish the opposite. ## What the primary evidence establishes

NVIDIA's June 2024 FAQ, questions 5, 10, 12, 17 and 18, distinguishes June 6 record ownership, nine additional shares distributed after the June 7 close, and expected split-adjusted trading at the June 10 open. It says a sale on or before June 7 relinquishes the additional shares regardless of settlement date. It also warns that brokerage availability can vary. These statements support trade-date economic entitlement rather than an enduring additional claim retained by someone who sold before the boundary. They do not certify an individual account's delivery. The document identifies June 11 as the **cash dividend** ex-date, which must not be confused with split-adjusted trading. [NVIDIA FAQ](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_downloads/2024/06/nvidia-2024-stock-split_faq_investors.pdf).

[FINRA Rule 11140](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/11140), paragraphs (a), (b)(2), (b)(3) and (c), recognizes exchange/Committee-designated ex-dates, places distributions/splits of at least 25% on the first business day after payable, and retains special handling for foreign securities and late information. It supports checking a designated event date rather than deriving permission from one vendor field. It does not establish this vendor record's correctness.

[FINRA Rule 11630](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/11630), paragraphs (a), (c), (e) and (f), describes due-bills as evidence transferring security rights or a seller's delivery obligation; it addresses pre-ex trades delivered too late for record registration, redemption and failure. This supports the interpretation that due-bills can carry ordinary trading entitlement across record-date mechanics. It also shows why omitting settlement is an abstraction with limits, not evidence that settlement obligations do not exist.

The relevant settlement regime is historical. [FINRA Notice 24-04](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/24-04), published February 26, 2024, makes the T+1 changes operative May 28, 2024; its discussion explains the revised small-distribution ex-date rule. June 2024 falls after that transition. Do not apply today's record-date conventions to earlier events; archive the event-relevant rule/version and calendar. The large-distribution rule is separate from the small-distribution T+1 change.

Official clearing corroboration is available: [OCC memorandum 54623, May 23, 2024](https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemos?number=54623), page 1, gives NVDA's 10-for-1 ratio, June 6 record, June 7 payable and June 10 ex-distribution date. This is a primary options-clearing notice, not a Nasdaq common-stock trading notice or broker delivery certificate. Targeted NasdaqTrader searches did not identify a common-stock notice; no exchange-notice verification is claimed. The separate Alpaca narrative URL `https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/corporate-actions` returned a web-tool accessibility error; the accessible reference and pinned schema below were used instead.

## Provider meanings and uncertainty

The [official pinned Alpaca schema](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alpacahq/cli/709d11400129dc7c09b41dd05263187e15801a71/api/specs/market-data-api.json), commit `709d11400129dc7c09b41dd05263187e15801a71`, has these `components.schemas` entries:

| Field | Documented meaning, paraphrased | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ex_date` | Shareholder-credit cutoff | Reconcile with designated split-adjusted trading. |
| `record_date` | Ownership date for the benefit | Not a complete regular-way entitlement algorithm. |
| `payable_date` | Benefit payment/distribution date | Preserve separately from issuer distribution and trading. |
| `process_date` | Alpaca processing date | Not issuer effectiveness or information availability. |
| `due_bill_redemption_date` | Due-bill obligation redemption | Not a documented declaration of economic-model equivalence. |

`forward_split` requires identity, rates, process and ex fields, but not record/payable/redemption. Its illustrative SRE record has redemption one day **after** ex. Therefore neither presence nor equality is a universal supported-split rule. Generic descriptions do not establish that vendor payable means broker processing, or that vendor dates equal issuer dates. The [Alpaca endpoint reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1) warns of creation/processing delays and specifies process-date search ordering. Current schema/reference documentation does not establish the fields' historical revision history or point-in-time knowledge.

## Required event reconciliation

I recommend a versioned judgment, mechanically bound to exact retained source bytes and raw record occurrences, with these independently checkable components:

1. **Identity and units:** provider event ID/category, symbol/CUSIP and issuer identity; positive old/new ratio with its direction; security class and currency; no unexplained identity transition or duplicate conflict. Establish issuer ratio independently of price movements.
2. **Distinct chronology:** retain provider ex, record, payable, process and redemption values with missing/null/empty distinctions; retain issuer record, legal effectiveness, distribution timing, first split-adjusted session, and any exchange/clearing dates separately. Include source URL, document date/version, retrieval/hash, locator, extracted assertion and attributed interpreter for every mapping.
3. **Entitlement rule:** cite evidence that ordinary pre-boundary buys carry the associated split interest and pre-boundary sales relinquish it, including trades after record date; identify the last cum-entitlement session and first ex/split-adjusted session. Record whether this is an express event statement or an inference from the issuer statement plus applicable rules. The inference must remain visible.
4. **Discrepancy disposition:** list each unequal date and whether it concerns different concepts, a documented vendor convention, a correction, or an unresolved conflict. A payable discrepancy cannot be called operational timing without documentary support. An unresolved provider record/payable discrepancy need not independently block economic-share accounting if event-specific primary evidence establishes every controlling economic fact, neither disputed field supplies a calculation input or entitlement inference, and no evidence connects the dispute to a different event or an exception affecting those facts. Preserve it as `unresolved_provider_issuer_discrepancy_outside_modeled_terms`, not reconciled, corrected or explained. This is an explicit model boundary, not a source factual claim. Refuse if any prerequisite is missing.
5. **Model scope:** transform economic holdings once, before the first adjusted session's open; treat the new units as including the related ordinary split entitlement. Do not separately credit the same distribution at record/payable/redemption. Record that this models regular-way beneficial exposure and assumes timely ordinary settlement, without modeling delivery, redemption, buying power, fails or broker availability. Preserve the original refusal and the post-acquisition policy revision.

The source adjudication should explicitly distinguish observed dates from the interpretation that an economic transformation represents regular-way entitlement. Hash binding establishes which bytes were cited; it does not certify that the interpretation is true. A separate reviewer must inspect the actual event judgment and source locators before admission.

## Refusal boundaries and applicability

The controlling economic facts are **identity, ratio, first adjusted trading session and the entitlement traveling with pre-ex regular-way trades**. A provider record/payable date is ancillary only when the accepted model never uses it and stronger event evidence establishes those facts despite the discrepancy. The unresolved difference must remain prominent in the admission record and result limitations; do not silently replace the raw date or assert a vendor convention.

Refuse conflicting ratio/security identity, conflicting designated first trading session, a date disagreement that undermines controlling economic facts, unexplained revisions affecting those facts, unclear entitlement for record-to-ex trades, or a separately tradable entitlement/when-issued market whose treatment is unknown. Also refuse evidence of a required election, contingent distribution, cash-in-lieu dependency, material delivery delay affecting the claimed model, or exceptional settlement conditions. A matching price discontinuity cannot cure missing source support.

Retain existing exclusions for specials, return of capital, foreign/unknown currency, coincident actions and identity ambiguity. Do not generalize this forward-split evidence to reverse splits, ADRs, distributions in other securities, rights, shorts, options, fractional broker processing or live execution. A nonempty redemption field is reviewable metadata under a deliberately accepted abstraction only after the complete reconciliation; no date-equality bypass is justified.

The available public evidence supports considering this narrow abstraction. It does not establish that the retained Alpaca event passes it, that provider coverage is complete, or that brokerage settlement is operationally safe. Those are distinct remaining checks; no historical calculation is authorized by this advisory report.
