## The boundary must precede acquisition

Current registration loads all source prices before recording exposure; `run_submission` validates admission and loads all normalized bars before selecting its calculation subset. Admission replays original pages across the complete acquired interval. Therefore, changing only evaluation dates cannot protect a suffix. Nor does an absent explorer exposure row establish nonaccess: acquisition and interrupted registration may already have created readable objects.

Add a durable reservation binding stable economic-security identity and aliases, market/feed/adjustment, inclusive development, final and warmup sessions, acquisition envelope, allowed purpose, source-use assessment, engine/specification/code identities, fixed cost scenarios, disclosure schema and authorized actors. Unknown security identity or overlapping unclassified acquisitions refuses protected status. Search exposures across aliases, revisions, raw/adjusted versions and relevant substitute instruments; unresolved alias relationships remain an explicit protection limitation.

Reservation, acquisition, inspection, calculation and release need distinct events. Before each page request, commit the reservation/lease binding. Store all protected responses, normalized objects, annotations, errors and derived results in the evaluator namespace; ordinary workers must receive neither paths nor readable object references. Partial and malformed responses are protected too. Existing development acquisitions remain unchanged, and importing any protected object into the explorer must permanently record breach/declassification rather than silently granting ordinary access.

Use a single transaction to reject overlapping reservations and allocate the one permitted final invocation. Bind a fencing token to its execution and publication. A crash after authorization leaves an uncertain consumed invocation requiring reconciliation; it must not create a fresh entitlement.

## Separate quality inspection from strategy feedback

A deterministic custodian can inspect prices for schema, coverage, source replay and financial admission inside the protected service. Its public receipt should disclose only a frozen allowlist: reservation identity, software/policy identity, completion state and fixed integrity codes. Exact missing dates, anomaly locations, action dates, price-derived quality statistics, returns and intermediate trade logs can reveal outcome-related information. Keep them protected unless the release policy explicitly permits them.

An independent data reviewer may require actual private evidence to resolve issuer identity or action semantics. Give that reviewer only the required protected material, record its identity and access scope, and exclude it from candidate selection and hypothesis revision for that reservation. Public calendar and issuer publications can be reviewed separately, but joining them to protected outcomes still belongs inside the boundary. If a reviewer also performs a research role, its exposure must follow the person/agent across role labels.

The five research roles should have durable assignments and exact input/output identities: director selects predeclared work; hypothesis researcher writes executable proposals; experiment engineer implements them; data/implementation critic reviews mechanics; statistical/portfolio critic reviews inference and selection. All can work on permitted development evidence. None receives protected prices or intermediate results before final release. Their tools should submit typed requests to local deterministic workers rather than accept arbitrary filesystem paths. Research-role separation does not replace independent quality and no-drift reviewers.

Do not assume a local CLI login makes model transmission local. Rights policy must separately specify what each model endpoint may receive. Until a source-use assessment authorizes a broader envelope, keep licensed prices and data-bearing reports out of all external packets, including the Fable/Opus council. Council review can examine synthetic fixtures, harness code, schemas and sanitized operational metadata. Dashboard conversation context must follow the same allowlist; a private document or status field is not safe merely because it contains prose.

## Admission and temporal accounting remain separate gates

Preserve D006/D007 source replay, full-acquisition calendar equality and event reconciliation inside the evaluator. Do not weaken action relevance to the displayed final window: inspect every acquired warmup/final session, unknown categories/dates and associated later payables. Existing `conditional_retrospective_development` admission cannot authorize final use. Add an explicit evaluation scope with independent source admission and reservation binding; the legacy explorer refuses that scope.

For a small first implementation, acquire separate development and protected datasets rather than slicing a shared readable acquisition. A protected acquisition can contain declared development warmup, but the entire response stays private; publish only the separately verified allowed development slice. This avoids accidental raw-page suffix leakage while retaining complete source evidence.

Freeze maximum warmup before reservation. Load exactly the declared preceding sessions and begin the evaluated portfolio in cash, matching current common-window semantics. Warmup overlapping development does not expose future final prices. When a split occurs inside final, rebase the signal's prior inputs only when effective; never preadjust development values using later events. Dividend claims and payables crossing boundaries follow the frozen economic policy, including unsettled terminal claims and first eligible cash use. Historical current revisions remain historical data, not historical point-in-time observations. An evaluator should refuse unsupported crossing events rather than shorten the window or move its boundary afterward.

## Consumption, replay and bypass controls

Record every bounded access request, including refused ones. A refusal proved to occur before protected reads consumes no price exposure, but still contributes to access history. Any read or computation using protected inputs consumes the authorized evaluation, even if the worker crashes, returns an error or publishes nothing. Outcome-sensitive admission failures themselves may leak information; retain detailed diagnostics privately and permit only fixed predetermined public failure states. Such failures cannot justify selecting another historical window as a fresh final test.

Exact deterministic replay may reuse the frozen invocation for verification under a distinct attempt identity; it never restores a final test. Changed rules, data, code or diagnostics-driven repairs require a new declared lineage and retain the original exposure. Statistical validity must account for the full predeclared candidate/cost family and prior evaluations, rather than treating each successful replay as a fresh experiment.

Concrete bypasses are `Provenance.read_bytes/read_json`, direct `data/objects` access, all acquisition functions/CLI commands, `register_dataset`, `admit_dataset`/`validate_admission`, suite registration/execution, direct engine imports, and arbitrary scripts. Shared journal outcome details and artifact references can also leak values or enable lookup. Gate acquisition and ordinary registration against the reservation registry; use evaluator-owned storage and typed capabilities for all protected reads; prevent raw protected engine calls by keeping their inputs inaccessible. A wrapper around `explore` alone is inadequate.

Current same-user native agents share owner filesystem authority. In that mode, signed role labels and capability checks provide cooperating-client controls only. Stronger protection requires a separate OS identity/service with evaluator storage and credentials unavailable to research processes, plus denied arbitrary tool execution against that identity. An owner/admin can still override that boundary; trusted external checkpoints and access audits address evidence, not universal secrecy. State the deployed threat model explicitly.

## Safe real-data pilot and acceptance criteria

First prove the lifecycle with synthetic fixtures, including corrupt pages, crashes, overlapping reservations, alias/revision overlap, direct API bypass, output leakage and split/dividend boundary cases. Independent critics must inspect the exact revision and reproduce refusals; independent quality must test storage separation and failure reconciliation.

Then reserve a small, explicitly application-protected historical interval outside existing acquired/exposed ranges, acknowledging unknown owner/model history. Freeze a mechanically selected instrument/window, cash-only rule, warmup, costs and release schema before bounded owner-authorized acquisition. Use local deterministic admission and execution; never print private tables or send them to council. Prefer a simple supported event profile, but do not search protected prices until a favorable or event-free interval appears. A failed pilot remains an attempt and consumes its access. Verify replay in the evaluator and release only the predetermined sanitized lifecycle receipt. This establishes access-control operation, not strategy merit. A subsequent prospectively reserved period can supply stronger temporal evidence once observations actually arrive; no future result should be claimed in advance.
