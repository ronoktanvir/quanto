## Decision

Implement a **single frozen champion, one final release, future interval** contract first. Continue historical development now; reserve an evaluation interval beginning after the candidate freezes. Previously acquired history remains development evidence permanently. An unfamiliar historical interval can support a qualified application-sequestered assessment, but cannot establish ignorance by the owner or a pretrained model. Waiting for future observations costs calendar time; pretending historical novelty would create stronger-looking but weaker evidence.

D008 should implement the contract and begin recording an actual prospective campaign, without claiming a completed evaluation. Statistical success must not be a prerequisite for building or exercising paper infrastructure. Operational paper observation of an unproven candidate is useful research, provided its status remains explicit.

## Freeze before access

The campaign manifest must bind hypothesis and economic rationale; family identifier and parent lineage; candidate rules and every parameter; instrument/universe eligibility; data/feed and revision policy; information availability; warmup, scoring and release dates; starting state; benchmark; signal/order timing; all costs and scenarios; primary estimand, minimum worthwhile effect, uncertainty method, risk limits and decision rule; sample plan; executable/source/dependency identities; reviewer identities; and publication permissions. No unspecified defaults may change after registration.

Use one champion and one primary benchmark for the first contract. Register the complete family search roster, including rejected, invalid, abandoned and unfinished submissions; preserve declared external searches as unverified disclosures. Separate candidate, execution, scenario and final-access counts. The 0/10/25-bps cases are three scenarios, not three independent discoveries; select the primary case before access and retain the others as sensitivity results. They remain assumptions, not calibrated transaction costs.

Require development-only chronological validation before freezing. All fitted transformations and model selection use earlier observations. Warmup may precede the scored window, but overlapping labels or outcomes must end before its boundary; apply an explicit gap when needed. Ordinary trailing prices alone do not require an invented universal embargo.

## Consumption and leakage

Give an evaluator service sole application access to reserved observations and results; researchers receive status metadata until the fixed release. Commit a durable reservation and consumption event before any final-data read, diagnostic, admission inspection or worker launch. A crash after that point consumes the opportunity, including when no result reaches the researcher. A failed pre-access validation remains recorded but need not consume an untouched interval. Unknown access state consumes conservatively.

Exact replay may recover the frozen campaign using identical candidate, data and code, with a distinct linked execution attempt; it is not another independent test. A correction changes identity and yields a diagnostic rerun, never restored final status. Admission discoveries requiring new accounting policy terminate the campaign as invalid; preserve it, develop the correction on exposed observations, and reserve a genuinely later interval. This is expensive but prevents the D006/D007 post-acquisition adaptation from being disguised as prospective confirmation.

Track exposure across stable security identities, aliases, revisions, overlapping intervals, warmup, artifacts, prompts, dashboards and failures. Ordinary acquisition registration currently marks development exposure; the reserved acquisition route must enforce its distinct access rules rather than retroactively relabel those records. Unknown alias or exposure history cannot certify an interval. Treat direct owner filesystem access as outside the application guarantee. Persist model/prompt identities and prior-knowledge disclosures; model training ignorance cannot be proven. Freezing before future observations removes one leakage route, not survivorship, stale data, economic regime change or accounting error. Adaptive holdout reuse can itself overfit; merely hiding raw prices is insufficient ([Dwork et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629)).

## Statistics and verdicts

For the first supported outperformance profile, use daily net strategy-minus-benchmark equity returns on identical sessions and capital. Freeze a positive minimum worthwhile daily difference `delta`, then test whether the mean difference exceeds it; report compounded wealth separately. A downside-control hypothesis needs a separately specified objective, not a post-result switch to whichever metric wins. Benchmark accounting must share action, timing and cash assumptions.

Recommend a one-sided confidence bound using a fully specified Newey–West long-run variance estimator, fixed lag-selection rule and documented weak-dependence assumptions. Independently validate its implementation and finite-sample behavior before use. HAC estimation addresses dependence under conditions; it does not create an exact small-sample guarantee ([Newey–West](https://www.nber.org/papers/t0055)). Report net wealth, drawdown, turnover, fees, exposure, trades, return observations, confidence bounds and sensitivity cases. Sharpe may be descriptive; naive square-root annualization and independent-observation precision are inappropriate when dependence matters ([Lo](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=377260)).

Predeclare a fixed sample length using development-only dependence/variance estimates and a power analysis at an explicitly specified alternative above `delta`. Recommend 80% planned power and a 252-session operational floor as policy choices, not sufficient evidence by themselves. If the required horizon is impractical, disclose low power and retain an inconclusive outcome; do not shrink `delta`, extend after seeing results or stop when significant. Trade count alone cannot establish adequacy.

Reserve campaign-wise error allowance before access: for successive registered final campaigns, a simple initial policy is `alpha_j = 0.05 / 2^j`, starting at `j=1`, with no reset through renamed families. This controls a cumulative bound only if each conditional test is valid; approximate dependent-return inference warrants a qualified claim. More efficient sequential designs can be separately reviewed later. Development trial counts inform search-risk disclosure; do not mechanically feed incomplete application counts into a Deflated Sharpe Ratio and call it certification ([Bailey–López de Prado](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)).

Keep separate axes: integrity (`valid`, `invalid`, `exposed`); statistical result (`criterion_met`, `criterion_not_met`, `inconclusive`); and operational readiness. Failure to reject is not “NO EDGE.” Criterion met means support under this registered model and interval, not established profitability.
