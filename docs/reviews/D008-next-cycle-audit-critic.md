# D008 next-cycle auditor criticism

**Hold actual use of the frozen auditor.** Its full-trace checks connect seven ordered stages to a newly registered comparison, three distinct financial children and nine independently reconstructed operands. Four gaps affect acceptance of an actual cycle. The auditor needs a prospective launch binding, exact broker outcome equality, stronger model and budget evidence checks, and an outer deadline covering its entire invocation.

## Bind the actual launch and complete returned outcome

`extra_trace()` verifies the hash at a specified event, a successful suite registration at or before that event and a broker start afterward. These checks establish event order. The packet supplies the checkpoint values after the run and carries no binding to a previously consumed launch. On the frozen invented full trace I reconstructed event 46 after completion and the auditor accepted it. Event 46 was both the suite-finish event and the immediate predecessor of the broker start in this case. No second distinct eligible cut is claimed.

Use the supervisor's prospective receipt chain as the real-mode authority. Before accepting completion, verify the committed plan and attempt, its consumed receipt, the exact original request, paid launch, accepted registration result and its mapped suite, paid worker consumption, prelaunch checkpoint and `paid-call-entered` bindings. The parent and worker should identify the same request bytes, run key, ordered failure roster, suite, journal head and authorization attempt. Verify the owned process/exit/result receipts and clean terminal outcome for a completed verdict. A missing or uncertain terminal remains a separately inspectable incomplete attempt. Bind this auditor's one allowed attempt to that plan so changing an audit ID cannot substitute another generation. The resulting provenance is for a trusted cooperating supervisor.

The original outcome check also accepts a proper subset of the broker's returned document. Both `context_checks.inspect()` and `trace_workflow()` compare only the fields present in the supplied receipt. In the invented full trace the original contained 17 fields. Supplying just `submission_id`, `artifact_sha256` and `status` passed both inspectors with seven stages, three children and nine operands. Require equality of the complete appropriate terminal return schema with the durable broker record, together with the exact supervisor-captured outcome bytes and digest. Preserve distinct exception and pending shapes.

## Verify model and spending evidence against their events

The auditor reconstructs the sent body and parsed decision, checks the requested/observed model against the exact stage contract, and joins effect, reservation, allocation and dispatch rows. Its per-run accounting excludes unrelated requests. Two independent in-memory mutations show where the current joins stop.

- Moving one request's reservation, purpose allocation and dispatch day together to `1999-01-01` passed both trace inspectors with the original journal chain intact. Their agreement with each other hid their disagreement with the `budget_reserved` event. Join the exact day and amount to that event, the dispatch event and authorization time in America/Los_Angeles. Match each terminal settlement or uncertain hold to its event and retained effect. Verify the supervisor's launch-day global and research-purpose headroom at the prospective cut. Separate the project's conservative local accounting from provider invoicing and unrelated account activity.
- Giving one private response a new content-addressed object with an unrecognized `response_hash_kind` and an invalid `response_sha256` still passed both inspectors. Require the transport-specific fingerprint kind, a valid digest and the retained response identity. Check bounded usage and its charge against the frozen pricing calculation, public/private receipts and settlement event. Where the HTTP body is unavailable, the claim ends at the digest recorded by the trusted transport and matched observation metadata.

The fixture branch and actual branch share these field joins. All witness data and model responses were synthetic. The model, ordering, failure-context, stage/effect and operand checks exercised by this review support the lineage of those records. They cannot supply the missing prospective or accounting evidence.

## Enforce the deadline through terminal acceptance

`audit_cycle.run()` starts its clock after loading sources, checking the environment, reading inputs and creating the attempt directory. It has no parent alarm and no final deadline check before accepting the result. Timeout cleanup can start a fresh three-second wait in the normal and exception paths. The numerical helper has a similar repeated wait. A launcher-only simulated-clock witness excluded 700 seconds of preflight and accepted a terminal result at reported elapsed time 900 seconds under the 600-second budget. It used an invented process handle and launched no child.

Use an absolute work deadline from entry, one absolute cleanup deadline and remaining-time checks for the worker and every oracle subprocess. Own and reap each handle once, check time before final acceptance, and retain an uncertain outcome when timely termination cannot be observed. Preserve partial receipts and avoid inferring quiescence from a result file. These are ordinary-process scheduling bounds.

## Projection and partial traces

The independent shape validator accepted `review_outcome=method_no_blockers` together with one blocking review. The normal frozen child derived a consistent review summary from the underlying trace. The weakness is in the parent's second check. Require agreement among the review outcome, published count and blocking/inconclusive counters, and ensure the parent's expected fixture mode agrees with the returned evidence class. The numerical total can also be checked against the three per-child terminal receipts. For a partial workflow, only completed checks should be counted. The existing auditor correctly withholds full acceptance and runs its three numerical comparisons only after all seven stages pass. A financial child left by an earlier stop needs a separately bound partial audit if its arithmetic is to be accepted.
