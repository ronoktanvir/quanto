## What the budget evidence establishes

The worker captures its planning snapshot before dispatch. The auditor verifies its event head, allocation prefix, limit, reported local remaining amounts and USD2.625 planning requirement. Each research authorization must fall on the planned Los Angeles day. A later settlement may fall on a later date. A research authorization has an atomic allocation, reservation and dispatch. For an ordinary provider call the allocation can precede its reservation in a separate transaction, including across midnight. The later reservation supplies an upper bound for the allocation date.

The corrected verifier requires a real, canonical YYYY-MM-DD date on every allocation. It checks that date against the allocation's own reservation when available, the planning cut and independent time bounds from surrounding rows in append order. An unproven prefix returns incomplete. Matching journal and row records can establish an ordinary charge's amount and a recognized purpose. Only confirmed conversation and operations charges are safely excluded from research on purpose alone. Missing evidence and unsupported labels leave research spending possible.

For the proven prefix the auditor records the minimum and maximum possible planning-day research exposure, how many relevant rows are ambiguous, the research spending attributed by the captured rows, and remaining purpose budget under the maximum. It refuses an impossible date, contradictory snapshot or established shortage. If eligible spending in the most conservative case cannot meet the planning requirement but remains possible in the least expensive case, it reports incomplete. A legitimately earlier allocation is allowed when the evidence supports it. An independent earlier-day reservation can exclude that allocation from the planning day altogether.

In the invented USD1.50 cases, both snapshots attributed zero to research and reported USD4 available. The evidence allowed USD0 to USD1.50 of planning-day research, leaving as little as USD2.50. At USD0.25 the same ambiguity left at least USD3.75 and allowed the audit to continue. A separate USD18 standalone reservation illustrated the global limit. Its canonical day must match the Los Angeles day of its journal timestamp. Charging it to an earlier day on paper could inflate apparent global availability from USD2 to USD20. A genuine prior-day event remained permissible. These bounds refer to recorded local amounts and the cooperating program's event and transaction rules. They cannot establish unrelated use of the same provider account.
