## Original visible response

# D008 Independent Policy Critique

**Basis.** This critique uses only the proposal text and the lead's attributed summaries. I have not inspected the adviser reports, the code, the tests, private data or prices. I cannot verify the cited SHA256 digests. The summaries describe near-unanimous agreement among two model families, all written by one lead. That uniformity should be recorded as a correlation risk, not as corroboration. This critique authorizes nothing.

## Consequential blockers

1. **"Reversible implementation" is undefined.** Several permitted actions cannot be undone:
   - paid model dispatches
   - registered attempts
   - failure-memory entries
   - pilot reservation and consumption

   **Repair:** list exactly what may proceed before council dispositions, for example local code and synthetic fixtures only. Every paid dispatch, registered development attempt or reservation would then wait for recorded dispositions.

2. **Paper operation can contaminate the future protected interval.** Prospective paper trading observes the same future sessions a frozen champion would later be evaluated on. If roles, the dashboard or the owner see paper outcomes and revise or select candidates, those sessions are exposed.

   **Repair:** choose one of two rules and record it before paper starts:
   - paper outcomes for any reservation-eligible candidate go through the evaluator boundary; or
   - any paper-visible interval is declared development-only and ineligible for protected status.

3. **The pilot may produce selectable results.** An access-control pilot that runs a candidate generates performance values, even if they are labelled "not efficacy."

   **Repair:**
   - fix the selection mechanism and its random seed before any data is requested;
   - prove "outside existing acquisitions" across aliases, revisions and feeds before the request;
   - release only integrity and lifecycle states;
   - forbid using pilot metrics for any later selection.

4. **The reviewer/selector separation is missing.** The data-boundary adviser required that anyone who reviews protected facts cannot also select the candidate. The proposal never states this rule.

   **Repair:** add it to the authorized-actor freeze. Also record that a shared lead or model makes the separation partial.

5. **Accounting for the OAuth-based council is unspecified.** The council path uses local OAuth and sits outside the API cap. The operations and roles advisers require unknown CLI billing to be preserved.

   **Repair:**
   - state whether council spend counts toward the USD 20/day cap;
   - reserve the worst-case cost per assignment before dispatch;
   - define "remaining budget" for Opus as atomic reservation headroom, not observed spend;
   - include council calls in uncertain-billing records.

## Precise corrections

- **Outbound packets need a deterministic gate.** "Expressly allowlisted" must name an owner who approves the allowlist and a typed packet schema. Add a deny-by-default check before transmission that rejects numeric series, dates within data ranges and provider identifiers. Log rejections without their content.

- **"Genuine" role work needs an operational test.** Acceptance should require that role output materially determined the new registered calculation. Examples are a changed rule, parameter or falsification criterion, traceable from the researcher's proposal to the engineer's specification to the supervisor-bound attempt. A supervisor-authored specification countersigned by models should not count.

- **Long-running effects need lease semantics.** State what happens when a lease expires while a deterministic study is running: the study is fenced off at publication and the attempt is marked uncertain. Also state whether heartbeats extend the fence.

- **The council transport needs explicit validation.** Adopt the roles adviser's checks:
  - no `--bare` flag;
  - validated terminal model identity;
  - tools, customizations and MCP verified disabled from transcript evidence, not configuration intent.

- **The external checkpoint needs a location, cadence and integrity method.** Without them, restoring from an older backup can silently undo consumption records and uncertain holds. Document this as a known owner-rollback limitation in addition to detection.

- **The statistical plan must be reviewed before the candidate freezes.** The estimand, power calculation, horizon, effect threshold and campaign error allowance must pass independent calibration review before the freeze. Reviewing them later forces diagnostic lineage. Maintain an error-allowance ledger across campaigns so "decreasing alpha" can be audited.

## Residual risks, acknowledged but not closed

- Same-user controls protect only cooperating callers. The owner, and any model with file access, sits outside the guarantee.
- Critics drawn from one provider and lead share failure modes. Splitting work between Haiku and Opus is not independence.
- Owner and model exposure to history before D007 cannot be ruled out. Historical protection is therefore only an access-control demonstration.
- At USD 4/day, real five-role acceptance may stall indefinitely. "Pending" must not drift into an informal pass.
- The design adds failure points, such as the lack of an embedding service, egress restrictions and strict schemas. These may leave roles too little to reason about, producing compliance theater rather than research.
- Uptime that depends on the Mac being awake and signed in prevents any unattended-operation claim.

## Acceptance conditions (in addition to those in the proposal)

1. A written list of what may proceed before dispositions, with all irreversible actions gated.
2. An exposure rule for the paper-trading interval, recorded before paper starts.
3. Pilot selection mechanism, overlap proof and integrity-only release schema frozen before any request.
4. Reviewer/selector separation added and enforced through issued assignments.
5. Budget reservation covering both API and council paths, with uncertain charges retained after crashes.
6. Egress validator tests, including adversarial packets containing dates and prices, that show refusal before transmission.
7. A traceable chain from role output to a materially changed registered attempt.
8. A restore test showing that consumption, holds and dispatch entitlements survive rollback, or that rollback is detected against the external checkpoint.
9. Recorded Fable and Opus dispositions, with any dissent preserved verbatim rather than summarized by the lead.

Until conditions 1 through 5 are met, I would treat D008 as design-approved for local and synthetic work only.
