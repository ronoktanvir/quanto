## Acceptance sequence and boundaries

Treat these as separately reviewable gates:

1. **Operational research workflow:** all five roles complete the bounded real-data workflow with journal-derived identities, capability checks, full attempt lineage, retrieved prior failures, genuine artifact-linked objections, and distinct quality and scope reports. Dashboard assignments, status, evidence, costs, freshness, and dependencies must derive from those records; it must label contextual chats separately and disclose that native-worker telemetry is lead-recorded.
2. **Protected access control:** synthetic denial, alias/overlap, concurrent one-use reservation, crash, replay, correction, leakage and restart cases pass independent criticism and quality review. Then complete a reserved historical access-control pilot without exposing protected values to roles or the dashboard. State the cooperating-client threat model: same-user filesystem administrators can bypass local controls, so do not promise absolute secrecy.
3. **Durable operation:** schedule and resume bounded research across restart; fence stale workers at every effect/publication boundary; reconcile uncertain provider calls without unbudgeted retries; search failures and fixes; verify backup restoration with intact artifact identities and histories.
4. **Prospective paper:** timestamp signals before eligible execution, model costs and constraints explicitly, reconcile orders/positions/cash, retain misses and interruptions, and freeze the observation horizon. Experimental paper observation may begin for operational learning without implying evidence-based promotion.
5. **Live readiness:** only after a concrete strategy is reviewed with appropriate evidence, limits, isolation, emergency stop, restart and reconciliation checks. Research roles and credentials stay separated. Explicit owner approval remains required to activate actual orders; an available but inactive adapter is not live trading.

Each gate needs revision-linked criticism, independent quality evidence, and a separate scope disposition. Update requirements and status only for demonstrated outcomes. Continue to mark unavailable outside-model review as pending, preserve dissent, and use the owner's existing council route and shared daily budget; no new subscription or purchase is justified by this scope.

## Product transparency and conclusion

The local website is an operator surface, not evidence that the operation is autonomous. Show actual assignment state, latest durable handoff, reviewer findings, blocked dependencies, data/evidence kind, last successful observation and next scheduled attempt. Distinguish planned work from dispatched work and completed work; represent unknown freshness or cost as unknown. A periodic status capture is a status observation, not agent progress. Keep dashboards and role conversations behind the same evidence allowlist and never expose protected prices, result tables, private strategy text, or licensed data to a chat context by default.

The decisive scope test is whether each increment reduces a named gap in R10–R18 and leaves evidence another reviewer can inspect. The near-term priority is to make the operation do one real, bounded research cycle while building a genuinely separate evaluator boundary. This prevents both forms of drift: declaring the prototype complete because its interface and historical pilot work, and postponing useful operational progress until a future statistical result exists.
