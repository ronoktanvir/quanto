# Alpaca backup permission for bars and corporate actions

On November 3, 2026, Los Angeles time, the owner reported that Alpaca had approved the arrangement they asked about. The question specified an encrypted backup of Alpaca historical bars and corporate-action data used for private, noncommercial research on the owner’s own account. The owner would encrypt on the Mac before transfer to a private Runpod volume, retain sole control of the decryption key, and restore the data only after a loss. Sharing and redistribution were outside the request.

The evidence is the owner’s report of Alpaca’s reply. The original wording and any feed-specific qualifications have not been inspected. The owner should keep the correspondence privately with their account records. This updates the earlier observation that a reply was pending. [Initial selected-use review](D009-backup-data-use.md).

Apply this clarification to the two named data classes when reviewing the archive manifest. Calendar files, other providers’ data, third-party source documents, private model responses and an entire research database need their own inclusion basis. The existing recovery inventory, key and Runpod account recovery, encryption, and downloaded restore checks remain governed by D009.
