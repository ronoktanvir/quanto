# D009 selected-use data check: private encrypted recovery

The proposed use is an encrypted recovery copy for the owner's own research account. Current public evidence does not establish the complete set of account and exchange terms governing that copy. This is an unresolved permission check, not a finding that all personal encrypted backups are prohibited. No market-data payload was uploaded during this review.

The lead revisited Alpaca's official documents on November 2, 2026. Its general terms describe personal, noncommercial use and restrictions on copying or transmitting content for publication, distribution or commercial enterprise. They also incorporate additional account agreements. Those provisions should be read with their stated purpose qualifications; they do not by themselves settle the specific owner-controlled encrypted-backup arrangement. ## Effect on this increment

The offline capture module can be implemented and tested with invented fixtures without resolving this external-retention question. It must not equate a locally hash-verified inventory with permission to transmit that inventory. Its initial scope contains no uploader or automatic egress route.

Before a real external copy, the selected payload needs an explicit use review covering source terms, private derived research, retained model responses and audit evidence. An encrypted file remains a copy; encryption limits who can read it but does not determine contractual permission. Conversely, a restriction on public distribution must not be silently rewritten as a ban on every private backup.

The required next evidence is the applicable account/exchange terms addressing personal retention and external encrypted storage, or clarification from the provider if those terms leave the use unresolved. Do not contact the provider without the owner's explicit messaging authorization. Any omission from the eventual archive must be recorded and assessed for whether the remaining copy can actually recover the research state. Excluding essential evidence cannot produce a complete-recovery claim.

This report changes no acquisition entitlement, budget, source license, upload permission or trading authority. It does not replace the broader Stage B key-custody, capture, verification and isolated-restore gates in D009.
