## Objections and disposition

**Loaded helper and policy identity was initially incomplete.** The initial wrapper bound disk bytes and selected entry points, which did not bind the helper functions and globals those entry points used. A changed selector or reducer policy could therefore disagree with a recorded freeze. The producer added complete loaded campaign/reducer helper and global fingerprints using the existing identity machinery. An independent final-source probe changed the selector helper, metric helper and metric year constant separately: each changed identity, and restoration recovered the exact original identity. The probe also retained the correct winner for a difference beyond ordinary Decimal precision under both default and precision-six contexts. Exact rational selection now avoids context-sensitive subtraction. An earlier probe overlapped edits and is not used as acceptance evidence; the final probe had identical before/after source hashes (terminal `da9ca9`).

**The local deadline originally admitted late dispatch and publication.** On campaign source `1a6a7f9c4cca40fb9c7f64b3828f2d6958b55c17c39f490a4b86002d22e9e1fc`, an independent invented-source probe advanced the clock during the real admission guard. Expiring during the first slot guard still launched one child; expiring during the final guard produced success and a summary at 301 seconds despite a 300-second limit (terminal `ebd09f`). The producer added checks after slow boundaries, around dispatch/reduction/selection, and inside the final publication transaction, including after `finish` before commit. Repeating the original probe against final source launched zero children on first-slot expiry and withheld the summary on final-guard expiry. Both outcomes were interrupted and both retries refused as consumed (terminal `31449e`). External process supervision is still required to interrupt a blocked operation; this local mechanism does not itself kill a process.

**Duration is a stated proxy, not exact elapsed investment time.** The reducer uses inclusive calendar labels and ACT/365.2425 for its growth annualization, suppressing it below a year. It does not infer precise open/close instants; a later block's starting wealth comes from the preceding recorded row while its duration starts at the block's first calendar label. This distinction was raised and is explicitly accepted and qualified in D011_POLICY. No exact execution-duration CAGR claim follows.

## Limits that continue to govern use

This accepts cooperating-process provenance and deterministic development calculations, not protection from a privileged local owner or arbitrary external-module replacement. The reducer is not a full independent replay of strategy transitions, corporate-action truth or provider completeness. Source and corporate-action reconciliation remain required for the actual observations. The toy arithmetic checks contain no empirical market evidence.

The one-use key applies to a frozen campaign identity. It is not an authorization to create additional campaigns until one wins. Later choices need the policy's explicit lineage and search accounting. The first acquisition must still follow the independently reviewed private freeze without substitution after refusal. Costs remain assumed per-fill scenarios, cash earns zero interest, and benchmark distributions remain cash; none of these choices establishes achievable execution quality or a universally conservative comparison.

Acceptance ends at the supervised development wrapper. Independent quality and scope checks, actual acquisition/admission, complete empirical cost reporting and independent arithmetic reconciliation are still separate work. The assessment block is not unseen data. Protected evaluation, future precommitment and elapsed observations remain incomplete, and no live execution is authorized by this report.
