## Minimum practical campaign

The private manifest should contain the complete intended roster before acquisition or scoring: instrument identities and mechanical selection rule, feed and adjustment basis, requested coverage, common scored windows, warmup, exact candidate specifications, benchmarks, cost cases, metrics, selection rule, finite limits, and code/dependency identities. Freeze the hypothesis rationale and trial allowance as well; a later manual rule change counts as another tried hypothesis. Hashing parameters after a successful calculation is not preregistration.

The wrapper needs only a durable campaign registration, a finite ordered roster, a one-use claim for each slot, and an inspection/summary path. Each slot binds campaign identity, candidate, instrument, scoring window, cost, registered request and resulting child submission. Commit the claim before dispatch. If a process dies between claim and result, retain an uncertain slot and inspect the existing child. Do not automatically rerun it or infer that no calculation occurred. Preserve invalid, rejected, incomplete and failed outcomes beside successes. An incomplete roster cannot be reported as a successful full comparison.

For planning, `candidates × instruments × scored windows × cost cases` is the financial child count. Cost sensitivities and repeated benchmarks are not independent hypothesis discoveries. Separately count unique hypotheses, instrument/window choices, diagnostic variants and execution attempts. Even a small parameter search is a search. Freeze limits on acquisitions, pages, total bytes, run time and output before dispatch; do not expand a date range or substitute a feed merely to fill missing data.

Use one manifest-pinned source build for the acquisition/admission/campaign/engine/metrics path. Extend the recorded code identity to include the new wrapper and metrics modules; the current explorer identity cannot attest to files it does not index. Record interpreter and dependency identities, exact normalized/source receipts and calendar/action admission dependencies. A hash establishes identity, not correctness: the independent comparator must calculate from the bound observations without importing the producer's metric implementation.

The first summary should report the complete matrix, same-window benchmark, fees, turnover, terminal holdings and undefined statistics. Name fixed-bps scenarios as fee/cost sensitivity assumptions. They are not measured spread, opening-auction slippage or market impact. A candidate may survive these assumed costs while remaining unverified for achievable execution. Persist `historical_market_data`, `unprotected_development`, and false protected/investment-validation flags in the machine-readable result, not merely a prose disclaimer.
