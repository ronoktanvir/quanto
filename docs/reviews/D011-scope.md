## The minimum useful empirical deliverable

The first campaign should produce one complete private comparison table covering every frozen candidate and cost case on the same declared scored dates. It should contain net growth, risk and drawdown measures with their assumptions, benchmark differences, turnover, exposure, trade count, and explicit invalid/undefined outcomes. The public record can identify method families and evidence limits without publishing private strategy parameters or licensed data. A favorable row cannot be shown without the unsuccessful rows that share its search budget.

The table needs a reproducible supporting trace: source/admission identities, exact rule and code identities, registered attempt identifiers, chronological blocks, cost conventions, and an independently checked arithmetic result. The arithmetic checker must compare financial outputs, not merely confirm that the producer returned a successful status. Record native agents' concrete contributions separately from paid operational model roles; five agents discussing a plan do not constitute five agents having tested strategies.

Fix the finite experiment count before outcomes are available. A few lookbacks of the same SMA rule are one family with related variants, not evidence of broad coverage of the quant-theory sheet. This narrow first family is a sensible engineering choice because its accounting already exists. Volatility sizing and reversal can stay visibly queued until their distinct trading semantics are implemented. Do not quietly substitute a newly invented strategy or a different instrument after an unfavorable outcome or admission failure.

The immediate conclusion may be that no candidate merits further evaluation. Distinguish an invalid calculation, failure of the declared screen, a risk/return tradeoff, insufficient evidence, and a favorable descriptive result. A failed screening criterion does not prove that no possible edge exists, and a favorable historical point estimate does not establish one. The accepted statistical method must determine any stronger inference.

## The unseen-data requirement remains a separate acceptance case

The implementation observation is decisive: `reserve` returns `protected_eligible=false`, and `protected_acquire` explicitly refuses. The event engine declares `holdout_status=unprotected_development`. This is an unfinished capability, not a wording problem. Do not remove either limitation to make the new campaign appear complete.

For the historical screen, report that later chronological blocks were withheld from a specified fitting step only when the records establish that fact. Do not equate that narrower claim with lack of prior human/model exposure or with enforced protected evaluation. A separate evaluator requires reconciled scope, acquisition/read mediation, a frozen final candidate and test plan, reviewer separation, one-use consumption, and controlled release under D008. New folders, actors, datasets, or downloads do not reset exposure.

If the historical non-exposure claim cannot be established, a future precommitted campaign is a legitimate route. It requires time to collect sufficient evidence. The duration, candidate, costs, benchmark, estimand, uncertainty method and release schedule must be declared before the relevant observations; a promise to choose those details later is not a completed freeze. The existing rule that visible paper observations become development exposure still applies. A short operational paper run cannot stand in for an adequately sized strategy evaluation.

## Required corrections and boundaries

The proposal's phrase “a read-only data credential” is stronger than the established evidence. D005 records that paper credentials are not inherently read-only. Replace it with a requirement that the acquisition process enforces the reviewed read-only method/host/path allowlist and excludes credentials from research/model output. Credential capability and transport restriction are different controls.

The proposal properly labels fixed per-fill charges as cost sensitivity, but every result headline should retain that qualification. Passing assumed charges means surviving those modeled charges. It does not establish achievable opening fills, liquidity capacity, taxes, or realized brokerage costs. Those facts need additional evidence before an implementability claim.

A descriptive campaign may freeze rules and report measurements without claiming inferential acceptance. Before a protected final campaign freezes, however, D008 still requires the full estimand, threshold, horizon, uncertainty/calibration method and campaign error ledger. D011 must not defer that statistical work until after the candidate's final observations are seen. Adding new signal, benchmark, dividend-reinvestment, cash-interest, sizing or execution semantics also requires explicit scope and verification; reusing an engine does not make those changes implicit.

## What should not delay the first supervised screen

D010 client retrieval, a redesigned website, a broader machine-learning catalogue, options pricing, market-wide fundamentals, crypto coverage, and complete external disaster recovery are not prerequisites for this bounded supervised historical question. Preserve them as unfinished requirements. D009's external-checkpoint rule applies before unattended research; it should not be generalized into a new requirement that the owner store a key before supervised development can continue.

The genuine prerequisites are the frozen finite campaign, supported and admitted observations, correct accounting/timing/cost semantics, complete attempt logging, and independent checks of the actual result. Council review should resolve the material D011 decision once; routine implementation within that decision should then proceed under the existing review rules. Repeated proposal-only milestones would drift from the owner's request to test strategies.
